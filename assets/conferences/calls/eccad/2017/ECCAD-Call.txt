East Coast Computer Algebra Day 2017
April 29 2017
Wolfram Research in Champaign, Illinois, USA

#############################################

The East Coast Computer Algebra Day (ECCAD) is an annual one-day meeting for those interested in computer algebra and symbolic mathematical computation. It provides opportunities to learn and to share new results and current work in progress. The schedule includes prominent invited speakers along with contributed posters. Plenty of time is allowed for unstructured interaction among the participants. Researchers, teachers, students, and users of computer algebra are all welcome.   ECCAD-2017 will be hosted by Wolfram Research on Saturday, April 29, 2017
