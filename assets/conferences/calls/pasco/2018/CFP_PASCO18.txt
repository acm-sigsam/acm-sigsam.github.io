

CALL FOR PAPERS PASCO 2017

9th International Workshop on Parallel and Symbolic Computation
Vancouver, Canada, May 25, 2018

http://sigsam.org/PASCO/2018/

The International Workshop on Parallel and Symbolic Computation
(PASCO) is a series of workshops dedicated to the promotion and
advancement of parallel algorithms and software in all areas of
mathematical computation. 

PASCO 2018 will be held in conjunction with the IEEE International
Parallel & Distributed Processing Symposium (IPDPS). 

www.ipdps.org

PASCO will be run as a satellite workshop immediately following 
IPDPS.  The collocation of PASCO and IPDPS provides the opportunity
for computer algebra researchers interested in parallel computing 
to participate in one of the main parallel computing conferences
and for attendees of IPDPS to be exposed to problems in symbolic
computation.

Since PASCO will run as a one day workshop there is limited room
for papers/presentations.  Paper selection will be based on a
two page extended abstract with quick turn around.  Selected papers
may be extended to full length.



CONFERENCE TOPICS

All topics covered traditionally by PASCO including:

* Design and analysis of parallel algorithms for computer algebra
* Practical parallel implementation of symbolic or symbolic-numeric algorithms 
* Design of high-performance algebraic packages and systems
* Data representation and distributed data-structures
* Considerations for modern hardware and hardware acceleration
    technologies (multi-cores, GPUs, FPGAs) 
* Cache complexity and cache-oblivious algorithms for computer algebra
* Parallel implementations of computer algebra algorithms on GPUs
* Parallel algorithm implementation and performance tuning
* Compile-time and run-time techniques for automating optimization
  and platform adaptation of computer algebra algorithms
* Applications of high-performance computer algebra in
  theorem proving, cryptography, computational biology, number theory,
  group theory, satisfiability checking, SAT solving, etc.


SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS

The conference invites submissions presenting original research.  
Submissions in the form of extended abstracts (2-4 pages) single-spaced 
double-column pages using 10-point size font on 8.5x11 inch pages 
(IEEE conference style), including figures, tables, and references. 
The submitted manuscripts should include author names and 
affiliations. See style templates for details:

http://www.ipdps.org/templates/IEEECS_CPS_LaTeX_Letter_2Col.zip

Papers are exclusively submitted via easy chair at

       https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=pasco2018

Papers must be written in English.  Papers must contain original research
and may not duplicate work published or submitted for publication elsewhere.
Papers will be reviewed by the Program Committee and external referees.

Authors of selected abstracts will be invited to extend their submission
to full paper (upto 10 pages).  All accepted papers will be distributed at 
PASCO 2018 and includedin the IPDPS proceedings.

At least one author of each accepted paper must attend PASCO 2018 to present
his or her paper.
