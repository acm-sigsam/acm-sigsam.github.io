notes:
- The conference series guide of the previous SIGSAM site lists this meeting as “MKM 2002”; the 2nd MKM conference (Bertinoro, LNCS 2594) was held on 16-18 February 2003.
- The old SIGSAM site lists this meeting as 'MKM 2002, Bertinoro, Italy' (with the link to the mkm03 pages and LNCS 2594); the official pages date it February 16th - 18th, 2003. There was no MKM conference in 2002 (the MKM Interest Group meetings page lists only NA-MKM 2002, McMaster University, Hamilton, Ontario, Canada, June 12-13, 2002, among 'Other MKM Meetings').
- Proceedings in the Springer-Verlag Lecture Notes in Computer Science series (home page); the volume number and DOI link are from the old SIGSAM site.
- Affiliated meetings of the MKM-Net and MoWGLI projects followed the conference (programme page).
- 'The other MKM meetings of 2003 listed by the MKM Interest Group (not part of this series): Mathematics On The Semantic Web, May 2003; Mathematical Knowledge Management Symposium at Heriot-Watt University, 25-30 November 2003.'
