notes:
- The main track had invited talks by Chee K. Yap, Alan Bundy and Zhi-Hua Zhou; Xiaoyu Chen, Cezary Kaliszyk and Guoliang Li gave invited talks in the special tracks ("Intelligent Documents" and "Collective Intelligence").
- 18 accepted papers and two invited papers appear in LNAI 11110 (eds. Jacques Fleuriot, Dongming Wang, Jacques Calmet). Supported by the Suzhou Institute of Beihang University.
- The call for papers names Wenjun Wu as chair of the organising committee; it lists the previous five meetings as Sevilla, Paris, Birmingham, Beijing and Linz.
- No AISC meeting was held in 2016 (AISC 2014 was the 12th and AISC 2018 the 13th conference), and no meeting after 2018 was found.
