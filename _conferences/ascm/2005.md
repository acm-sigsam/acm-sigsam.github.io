notes:
- The 7-th ASCM, hosted by the Korea Institute for Advanced Study and sponsored by the Korean Mathematical Society and the Korean Federation of Science and Technology Societies.
- The meeting focused on computer algebra; 78 papers were scheduled. It was organized in 14 sessions, each run by two co-chairs (listed both under 'Session Chairs' and under 'sessions'); submissions were extended abstracts (2-4 pages) e-mailed to a session co-chair.
- 'Proceedings: the home page says a special issue of the Journal of Symbolic Computation was being planned for the conference (full papers due March 15, 2006). No proceedings volume or link is given; none recorded.'
- No invited or plenary speakers are listed on the site or the schedule.
- 'Dates page: extended abstract deadline September 30, 2005; registration with housing November 17, 2005.'
- The archived frameset notes it was saved from http://newton.kias.re.kr/ASCM2005/, which is recorded as the website; the dates and venue come from the banner image (head.files/title.png).
- The series site http://www.mmrc.iss.ac.cn/ascm/ now returns 404; all pages were read from Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) copies. Per the series page, ASCM was founded in 1995 by the Mathematics Mechanization Research Center (MMRC) of CAS and the Japanese Society on Symbolic and Algebraic Computations (JSSAC).
