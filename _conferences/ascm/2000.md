notes:
- Held jointly with the 5th Asian Technology Conference in Mathematics (ATCM'2000).
- Registration was at http://www.reg.cmu.ac.th/atcm&ascm2000 (stated on the call page).
- The call lists the invited speakers as Prof. Gaston H. Gonnet, Prof. Daniel Lazard (Université Pierre et Marie Curie, France), Dr. William McCune and Prof. Wen-tsün Wu; talk titles and the dates (Dec 18-21) come from the symposium program, which gives Lazard's affiliation as Université Paris VI, France.
- The symposium program page is archived under the 2001 directory (ascm01-prog.htm) but its title is 'ASCM 2000 Program'.
- The call says the proceedings will be published as a volume in the Lecture Notes Series on Computing by World Scientific and be available at the symposium. The DOI is from the World Scientific/Crossref record 'Proceedings of the Fourth Asian Symposium (ASCM 2000), Chiang Mai, Thailand'.
- Submission deadline June 30, 2000; notification August 15, 2000; camera-ready August 31, 2000.
- No general chair or local committee is named on the archived page.
- The series site http://www.mmrc.iss.ac.cn/ascm/ now returns 404; all pages were read from Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) copies. Per the series page, ASCM was founded in 1995 by the Mathematics Mechanization Research Center (MMRC) of CAS and the Japanese Society on Symbolic and Algebraic Computations (JSSAC).
