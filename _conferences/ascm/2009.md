notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Fukuoka, Japan”.
- Held jointly with the 3rd International Conference on Mathematical Aspects of Computer and Information Sciences (MACIS 2009), supported by the GCOE program 'Math-for-industry' of the Graduate School of Mathematics of Kyushu University and the Mathematical Research Center for Industrial Technology of Kyushu University. ASCM and MACIS had separate program committees and parallel sessions; invited talks were plenary and shared.
- All committees above are from the 'JOINT ORGANIZING COMMITTEE' page. The MACIS PC is included because it is part of the joint organization.
- Supported by Math-for-Industry Kyushu Univ., Fukuoka Univ., MRIT, SPARC Japan, CYBERNET, JSSAC, Microsoft, Maplesoft & JST.
- 'Change of invited speaker: the program page says the invited talk on linear boundary value problems with Groebner bases will be given by Dr. Markus Rosenkranz, a collaborator of Prof. Bruno Buchberger.'
- Three organized sessions (recorded under 'sessions'). The Digitizing Mathematics session was on 15 December 2009; its proceedings were to appear in the Math-for-Industry Lecture Notes series. The Validated Numerical Computation session had 6 invited talks.
- Accepted extended abstracts/full papers were printed for the conference; authors were invited to submit revised papers to a Springer LNAI volume. The 2009 and 2012 symposia share one Springer volume (DBLP record conf/ascm/2012, DOI 10.1007/978-3-662-43799-5).
- Submission deadline Sep 25, 2009 (extended); notification Oct 20, 2009; camera-ready 2009.11.10.
- 'The proceedings front matter (''Organizing Committee of ASCM2009'') agrees with the site: General Chair Masakazu Suzuki; Program Committee with Yosuke Sato and Chee Yap as co-chairs; Local Arrangements Tatsuyoshi Hamada, Koji Nakagawa, Hiroshi Yoshida. It writes ''Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science'' (Ziming Li) and ''Kyushu University'' (Mitsuhiro Nakao) where the site has ''Academy of Mathematics and System Sciences'' and ''Kyushu Univesity''; the site spelling is kept. Its preface lists financial supporters: Math-for-industry and Mathematical Research Center for Industrial Technology at Kyushu University, Ehime Campus Information Service, Cybernet, JSSAC, Maplesoft, and JAL Hotels. ''Conference Organization'' and preface taken from the front matter of the Springer proceedings volume (DOI 10.1007/978-3-662-43799-5), read from the publisher''s reading sample hosted at content.e-bookshelf.de (link.springer.com blocked automated access).'
- The website URL is the one given in the Digitizing Mathematics call (gcoe.math.kyushu-u.ac.jp/ascm-macis2009); the pages were read from the mirror on mmrc.iss.ac.cn via the Internet Archive.
- The series site http://www.mmrc.iss.ac.cn/ascm/ now returns 404; all pages were read from Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) copies. Per the series page, ASCM was founded in 1995 by the Mathematics Mechanization Research Center (MMRC) of CAS and the Japanese Society on Symbolic and Algebraic Computations (JSSAC).
