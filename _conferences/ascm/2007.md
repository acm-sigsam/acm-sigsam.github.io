notes:
- ASCM 2007 is the eighth in the series; organized with the NUS School of Computing (site hosted at comp.nus.edu.sg; the live site now returns 403, so the mirror on mmrc.iss.ac.cn was read via the Internet Archive).
- 'Sponsors: Lee Foundation of Singapore, Institute of System Science (China), Kim Seng Holdings Pte Ltd.'
- 'Disagreement on conference chairs: the Organizing Committee page (last modified 13 Nov 2007) lists Eng-Wee Chionh and Wang Huaxiong; the first call for papers lists Wang Huaxiong and Xing Chaoping. The committee page is used.'
- 'The call''s PC list also includes John Cannon (Univ. of Sydney, Australia), who is not on the committee page; affiliations in the call are partly worded differently (e.g. Miroslaw Majewski: Zayed Univ., Abu Dhabi, UAE). The committee page is used.'
- 'Called ''Keynote Speakers'' on the site. The program page words two titles differently: Farouki ''Minkowski geometric algebraa of complex sets --- theory, alg., applications'' and Xiaoyun Wang ''Some Mathematical Problems in Crypanalysis''. Titles are from the keynote page.'
- Pre-symposium proceedings in electronic form; selected papers in Springer LNCS/LNAI after the conference. The proceedings label and DOI are from the DBLP record conf/ascm/2007.
- 'Important dates (site): submission Sep 21, 2007 (the call said Aug 31); notification Oct 31; camera-ready Dec 1, 2007. Held alongside ATCM 2007 (linked under ''Other Conferences'').'
- The series site http://www.mmrc.iss.ac.cn/ascm/ now returns 404; all pages were read from Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) copies. Per the series page, ASCM was founded in 1995 by the Mathematics Mechanization Research Center (MMRC) of CAS and the Japanese Society on Symbolic and Algebraic Computations (JSSAC).
