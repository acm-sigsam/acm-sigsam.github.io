- 'Plenary speakers recorded as invited speakers. The ''Plenary Speakers'' page also lists speakers in four organized sessions (recorded under ''sessions''; organizers not named): Organized Session 1: On The Latest Progress In Verified Computation: Aurélien Greuet (Paris 6, France) - Exact Polynomial Optimization: Algorithms, Complexity and Implementation; Zhiqing He (East China University of Science and Technology, China); Nan Li (Chinese Academy of Sciences, China) - Verified Error Bounds for Isolated Singular Solutions of Polynomial Systems; Xuefeng Liu (Waseda University, Japan) - On High Precision Eigenvalue Estimation for Self-adjoint Elliptic Differential Operator and Its Application; Masaaki Kanno (Niigata University) - Towards Guaranteed Accuracy Computations in Control; Hiroshi Sekigawa (Tokai University, Japan) - Computing the Nearest Real Univariate Polynomial with a Real Multiple Zero and Its Application; Akitoshi Takayasu (Waseda University, Japan) - Verified Computations for
  Elliptic Boundary Value Problems on Arbitrary Polygonal Domains; Zhengyu Wang (Nanjing University, China) - Computer-Assisted Proof of Existence of Nash Equilibrium; Yimin Wei (Fudan University, China) - The Diagonal Reduction Algorithm Using Fast Givens; Zhengfeng Yang (China East Normal University) - Exact Safety Verification of Hybrid Systems Based on Hybrid Symbolic-Numeric Computation | Organized Session 2: Computational Geometry: Bernard Mourrain (INRIA Méditerranée, France) - Subdivision Methods in Geometric Modeling; Liyong Shen (University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, China) - Numerical Reparametrization of Rational Parametric Plane Curves; Xiaoran Shi (Beijing Computational Science Research Center, China) - Using μ-bases to Implicitize Rational Surfaces with a Pair of Orthogonal Directrices; Elias Tsigaridas (INRIA Paris-Rocquencourt, France) - Univariate Real Root Isolation in Extension Field and Applications to Topology of Curves | Organized Session 3: Parametric Polynomial
  Computations: Shutaro Inoue (Tokyo University of Science, Japan) - Parametric Approaches to Combinatorial Problems; Katsusuke Nabeshima (Tokushima University, Japan) - Algebraic Local Cohomology Classes Associated with Semi-quasihomogeneous Singularities; Yosuke Sato (Tokyo University of Science, Japan) - Stability of Gröbner Bases in Terms of a Commutative von Neumann Regular Ring; Dingkang Wang (Chinese Academy of Sciences, China) - Computation of Zero Divisors in Residue Class Rings of Parametric Polynomial Ideal; Kazuhiro Yokoyama (Rikkyo University, Japan) | Organized Session 4: Differential and Difference Algebra: Maximilian Jaroschek (Research Institute for Symbolic Computation, Austria) - Improved Polynomial Remainder Sequences for Ore Polynomials; Zhenbing Zeng (East China Normal University, China); Mingbo Zhang (University of Science and Technology of China, China) - Factorization of Differential Operators with Ordinary Differential Polynomial Coefficients'
