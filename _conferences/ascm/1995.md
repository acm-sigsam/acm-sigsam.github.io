notes:
- First Asian Symposium on Computer Mathematics. The preface says the series grew out of the Winter Workshop on Computer Algebra held in Tokyo, January 21-22, 1994.
- Sponsored by the Mathematics Mechanization Research Center (MMRC) and the Japanese Symposium on Symbolic and Algebraic Computations (JSSAC) (wording of the preface).
- Over forty participants from Japan, Korea, France and China; more than twenty papers presented, all collected in the proceedings.
- The ASCM'95 page reproduces the proceedings preface and table of contents only; no committees or invited speakers are listed.
- The series site http://www.mmrc.iss.ac.cn/ascm/ now returns 404; all pages were read from Internet Archive (Wayback Machine) copies. Per the series page, ASCM was founded in 1995 by the Mathematics Mechanization Research Center (MMRC) of CAS and the Japanese Society on Symbolic and Algebraic Computations (JSSAC).
