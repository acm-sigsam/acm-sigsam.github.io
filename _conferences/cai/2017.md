notes:
- The conference had five tracks, each with its own chairs and invited speaker; submissions were sent by e-mail to the track chairs. No program committee list is published.
- Zoltan Esik is marked as deceased in the steering committee list.
- Proceedings were published in electronic form (Book of Abstracts); best papers were invited to a special issue of Theoretical Computer Science (Elsevier). Extended abstracts of the Design Theory track were to appear in a special volume of Electronic Notes in Discrete Mathematics (ENDM).
- The conference hotel was the Elite City Resort, Navarinou 2, Kalamata; the website does not state explicitly that the sessions were held there.
