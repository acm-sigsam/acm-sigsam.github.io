notes:
- Sponsored by the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, the University of California at Berkeley and NASA Langley Research Center.
- The technical programme consisted of 45 contributed papers and a workshop meant to promote exchange of information between implementers and users of MACSYMA.
- The conference originated in an organizing session called by Joel Moses of M.I.T. at the 1976 ACM Symposium on Symbolic and Algebraic Computation (Yorktown Heights, August 1976).
- The foreword acknowledges the cooperation of ACM, ACM-SIGSAM, the Electronics Research Laboratory and the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences of the University of California, the Laboratory for Computer Science of M.I.T., NASA Langley Research Center and the U.S. Energy Research and Development Administration.
