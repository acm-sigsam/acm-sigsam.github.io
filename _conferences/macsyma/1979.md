notes:
- The proceedings of the Third MACSYMA Users' Conference (1984) say that the first conference was held at Berkeley in 1977 and the second in Washington, DC in 1979, and that the proceedings of the 1979 conference were published by the MIT Laboratory for Computer Science (edited by V. E. Lewis).
- The conference series guide of the previous SIGSAM site gives the place as "Schenectady, Washington DC"; Schenectady was the place of the 1984 conference.
- Bibliographic citations of papers in the proceedings give Washington, D.C., June 1979 (some give June 20-22, 1979); the exact dates were not confirmed from a primary source.
- No officers, committees or invited speakers were found online (the proceedings are not available online).
