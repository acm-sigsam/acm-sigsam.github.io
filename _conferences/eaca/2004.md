notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Santander, Spain”.
- Ninth Encounter; satellite meeting of ISSAC 04 (International Symposium on Symbolic and Algebraic Computation), held in Santander July 3-7, 2004.
- Summaries of less than 5 pages; submission deadline April 19, extended to April 26. Proceedings had 'the status of mere internal documentation for the Meeting'; accepted summaries could be published (with authors' permission) in the ACM SIGSAM Bulletin and the electronic journal TQ.
- David Cox's talk was 'Sponsorized by Addlink, Software Científico'.
- Registration, travel and lodging handled by AFID-Congresos (http://www.afidcongresos.com).
- Talk titles taken from the abstracts page (resumenes.htm), written in capitals there.
- The site (http://eaca2004.unican.es/, later https://historicosweb.unican.es/eaca2004/) is offline; content read from Internet Archive copies (2005-2006). The ISSAC 2004 page in website/issac/2004/others.html also describes EACA 2004 as held in Santander, July 1-3.
- 60 Spanish and 34 non-Spanish participants (Red EACA history page).
