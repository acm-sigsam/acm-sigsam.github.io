notes:
- The conference series guide of the previous SIGSAM site gives the location as “Valladolid”; the meeting's own pages and the series bibliography give Peñaranda de Duero, Spain.
- Eighth Meeting; hosted by the University of Valladolid but held at the Palacio de Avellaneda in Peñaranda de Duero (the series lists usually name it 'Valladolid (2002)'). First meeting of the biennial period (biennial since 2002).
- 'Sponsors: Universidad de Valladolid, Junta de Castilla y León (Consejería de Educación y Cultura), Real Sociedad Matemática Española, MCYT, Addlink, Sun.'
- The ConferenceSeries page of the site lists EACA 2002 without a link; the official site is http://www.cie.uva.es/eaca2002/ (also http://www.uva.es/eaca2002), offline; content read from Internet Archive copies (2003-2004).
- Proceedings record (Actas del EACA 2002) taken from the link given on later EACA websites; no online full text found.
- Abstracts published in the ACM SIGSAM Bulletin, as listed on the local SIGSAM site (website/cca/issues/issue143.html); the article PDF is not present in the local copy.
- 52 Spanish and 16 non-Spanish participants; the Red EACA history page names the meeting 'Peñaranda de Duero (EACA-2002)'.
