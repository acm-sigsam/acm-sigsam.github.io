notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Santiago de Compostela, Spain”.
- Twelfth Meeting, hosted by the Red EACA, partially supported by the Acción Complementaria MTM2009-06305-E/MTM.
- Five plenary talks proposed by the scientific committee; talk titles are not given on the archived pages (abstracts were linked as files).
- Selected papers were invited for publication in the journal Computing (Springer).
- 'Sponsors: Universidade de Santiago de Compostela, Universidade da Coruña, Consellería de Educación e Ordenación Universitaria, Ministerio de Ciencia e Innovación, RSME.'
- Program included a book presentation by Julio Rubio ('Miriam Andrés in memoriam') and a tourist and cultural visit to A Coruña.
- The book of abstracts (ISBN 978-84-9887-518-8) was found via web search in institutional repositories, not on the meeting site.
