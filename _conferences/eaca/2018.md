notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Zaragoza, Spain”.
- XVI Encuentro (bienal desde 2002), organized by the Instituto Universitario de Matemáticas y sus Aplicaciones (IUMA) of the Universidad de Zaragoza.
- 'Red-EACA partially funded by the Ministerio de Economía y Competitividad, Acciones de Dinamización ''Redes de Excelencia'' MTM2016-81932-REDT. Sponsors: MINECO MTM2016-81932-REDT, IUMA, RSME, Facultad de Ciencias (UZ).'
- Preceded by SAGE DAYS 94 in Zaragoza (28 June - 4 July 2018), https://wiki.sagemath.org/days94.
- Extended abstracts (at most 4 pages, English or Spanish); 28 contributions accepted after a standard referee process, plus 5 plenary talks (proceedings preface).
- The meeting website has no committee page; the committees are taken from the preface of the proceedings (Monografía 43), which names the Scientific Committee 'chaired by Francisco J. Castro (U. de Sevilla)' and a 'local committee' (no affiliations given; the editors are from IUMA, Universidad de Zaragoza).
- Plenary talks were held in the Aula Magna de la Facultad de Ciencias (Edificio A, Campus Plaza de San Francisco).
