notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Ezcaray, La Rioja, Spain”.
- Séptimo Encuentro, continuation of Santander (1995), Sevilla (1996), Granada (1997), Sigüenza (1998), Tenerife (1999) and Barcelona (2000).
- 'Two of the five plenary talks were joint: Eladio Domínguez and Luis Español; Laureano González-Vega and Rafael Sendra (each pair listed above with the shared title).'
- Pre-conference workshop (Actividades previas) by Francis Sergeraert on 11 de Septiembre de 2001 at the Departamento de Matemáticas y Computación, Universidad de La Rioja, Logroño.
- Deadline for papers 30-Abril; accepted abstracts were included in Actas 'que tendrán su correspondiente ISBN', distributed at the meeting as an internal working document; no online link found.
- 'Sponsors linked: Universidad de La Rioja, Gobierno de La Rioja, MCYT, RSME, Fundación Caja Rioja, Ibercaja, Addlink, Sun.'
- Original URL http://www.unirioja.es/dptos/dmc/eaca2001; now at https://www.unirioja.es/dptos/dmc/FueNoticia/eaca2001/.
- Abstracts published in the ACM SIGSAM Bulletin, as listed on the local SIGSAM site (website/cca/issues/issue138.html); the article PDF is not present in the local copy.
- 81 Spanish and 8 non-Spanish participants (Red EACA history page). That page gives M. Chardin's affiliation as U. Paris VII, while the meeting site says Institut de Mathématiques, CNRS, Paris (site version kept).
