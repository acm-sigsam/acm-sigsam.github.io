notes:
- Cancelled due to COVID19
- 'Cancelled: on March 18, 2020 the local organizing committee and the FoCM executive board announced that FoCM 2020 (and its satellite event SmaleFest, planned for June 21, 2020) was cancelled because of the COVID-19 pandemic.'
- On July 12, 2020 it was decided that the next FoCM conference would be held in 2023, to keep the usual three-year cycle, rather than rescheduling in 2021.
- From January 2021 the FoCM Society hosted an online seminar series with two lectures each month by plenary speakers of the FoCM 2020 conference.
- The plenary programme also listed a lecture by the Smale Prize winner; Afonso Bandeira (ETH Zurich) was awarded the fourth Stephen Smale Prize. The Vasil A. Popov Prize was also to be awarded at the conference.
- Organisers' affiliations are given on the detailed workshop pages and were not recorded here.
- Data from the FoCM 2020 site and the FoCM Society's conference list (October 2026).
