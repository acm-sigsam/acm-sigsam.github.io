notes:
- The scientific organization was administered by the Board of Directors of the FoCM Society together with the Local Organizing Committee; the Workshop Committee selected workshop topics and organisers, and the Plenary Speakers Committee chose the plenary speakers among nominated names.
- The conference site calls it the seventh in a sequence that began with the Park City, Rio de Janeiro, Oxford, Minneapolis, Santander and Hong Kong FoCM meetings.
- 'Sponsors named on the home page: National Science Foundation; International Mathematical Union, Commission for Developing Countries.'
- The plenary speakers page gives each speaker's subject areas, with links to abstracts; talk titles were not recorded. The workshop list has no workshop B3.
