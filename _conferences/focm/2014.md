notes:
- The conference site calls it the eighth in a sequence that began with the Park City, Rio de Janeiro, Oxford, Minneapolis, Santander, Hong Kong and Budapest FoCM meetings.
- 'Plenary periods: 11-13, 15-17 and 18-20 December 2014; the programme of the first period also lists a Smale Prize Lecture. The plenary speakers page gives subject areas, not talk titles.'
- The FoCM Society's conference list names Alejandra Rodriguez Hertz as main local organiser; the conference committees page lists Jana Rodriguez Hertz as chair of the Local Organizing Committee and Alejandra Rodriguez Hertz on the Plenary Speakers Committee.
- The workshops page of the conference site shows the workshop titles and organisers as images only, so the workshops were not recorded.
