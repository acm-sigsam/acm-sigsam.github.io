notes:
- The organisation was overseen by the FoCM Board of Directors; the day-to-day work was undertaken by the subcommittees listed.
- Workshops 1-6 ran on 5-7 August, 7-12 on 8-10 August and 13-18 on 12-14 August 2002.
- The meeting included a discussion panel on "Future of the foundations of computational mathematics", moderated by Endre Suli, with Steve Smale, Lenore Blum, Peter Olver and Ron DeVore on the panel.
- Talk titles of the plenary speakers are on separate pages of the archived site and were not recorded.
