notes:
- The meeting listed on the old SIGSAM site as "MMCP 2002" is the V International Congress on Mathematical Modelling (V ICMM); later MMCP sites list it among the previous conferences of the series.
- Organized by the Laboratory of Information Technologies of JINR, the Institute for Mathematical Modelling RAS and the Scientific Council of Mathematical Modelling of the Russian Academy of Sciences (headed by academician A.A. Samarsky).
- The program included 30 min plenary lectures, 15 min talks and posters, on 12 topics. The preliminary program (https://vicmm.jinr.ru/Prelprog.htm) lists the plenary talks with authors and titles run together on one line; they are not transcribed here.
- Selected papers were announced to be published in special issues of refereed journals; no proceedings volume is named on the site.
- Academician A.A. Samarsky is given as "Academician A.A.Samarsky (IMM RAS)" (Honorary Chairman).
