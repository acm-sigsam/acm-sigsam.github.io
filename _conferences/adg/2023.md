notes:
- '14th edition of ADG. Special focus topic: Deduction in Education; a one-day workshop (Workshop Deduction in Education, WDE) was integrated in the programme on 20 September.'

- The programme also includes a Guest Lecture 'Application of Quantifier Elimination in Geometry' by Žarko Mijajlović.
- 'Programme sessions were held at Hotel Palace (20-22 September). Host institution: Faculty of Mathematics, University of Belgrade, Serbia.'
- 'Sponsors: the Faculty of Mathematics of the University of Belgrade, the Serbian Ministry of Science, DataArt and Yettel.'
- 'CfP: extended abstracts (3 to 12 pages) via EasyChair; informal proceedings printed and online; authors of accepted abstracts invited to submit full papers to formal post-proceedings. The CfP notes that an AMAI special issue was organized for ADG 2021.'
- 'Important dates (CfP): abstracts June 5 extended to June 12; notification July 3 extended to July 10; final version September 4.'
