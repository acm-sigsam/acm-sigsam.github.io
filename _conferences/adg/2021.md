notes:
- 13th edition of ADG. It was supposed to be held in 2020 in Hagenberg, Austria (site https://www.risc.jku.at/conferences/adg2020/), but due to the covid pandemic it was postponed to September 2021; on 18 June 2021 it was decided that ADG 2021 would be held online.
- 'Special focus topic: links between automated deduction in geometry and robotics.'
- 'The CfP PDF (header: ''RISC, Hagenberg, September 15-17, 2021'') says in its body that ADG 2021 is co-organized with RISC and will be held in Hagenberg, Austria, July 13-15, 2021, or online/mixed if needed; the final pages give September 15-17, 2021, online.'
- Program Committee taken from the committees page (HTML-commented entries excluded). The CfP (web and PDF) additionally lists Fadoua Ghourabi (Ochanomizu University, Japan), gives Jose Capco as 'RISC, Johannes Kepler University, Austria', Armin Biere as 'FMV, Johannes Kepler University, Austria' and Georg Grasegger as 'The Johann Radon Institute for Computational and Applied Mathematics, Austria'.
- 'Important dates: abstracts May 31 extended to June 7; notification July 7; final version August 15; registration September 13.'
- All sessions were recorded and the videos are on YouTube (https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLi7gJjVg5rNDkl2HQdWm_C5eXVsvfJv-T).
- 'Sponsors: JKU - Johannes Kepler University Linz; RISC - Research Institute for Symbolic Computation; DK Computational Mathematics.'
- The ADG 2023 CfP states that a special issue in Annals of Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence (AMAI) was organized for ADG 2021.
