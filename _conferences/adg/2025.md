- 15th edition of ADG; held as a satellite event of CADE30 (https://www.dhbw-stuttgart.de/cade-30/).
- 'The overview and call pages list the invited talk title as ''T.B.A.''; the title above is taken from the programme page (''Invited Talk: Automating Geometry Construction Problems'').'
- Submission was via the CADE30 EasyChair (https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=cade30). Extended abstracts of 3 to 12 pages; informal proceedings on the conference web page; authors invited to submit full papers to formal post-proceedings (an AMAI special issue).
- 'Important dates: extended abstracts 9 of May (new deadline); notification 1 of June; conference 1, 2 of August 2025.'
- 'Programme: 4 sessions over two days with 16 contributed talks, one invited talk and a Business Meeting (chairs Pedro Quaresma and Julien Narboux).'
- The site reports 'A very successful edition' after the event. The venue within Stuttgart is not stated on the ADG pages.
