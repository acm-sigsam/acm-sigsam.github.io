notes:
- Organized by the Computer Graphics and Geometry Group of ICube Laboratory (CNRS / Université de Strasbourg); sponsored by the IGG team and the ICube laboratory.
- 'Dates: the website and the proceedings title page give June 27-29, 2016; the preface of the informal proceedings (and the HAL abstract) says ''held on June 26-28, 2016''.'
- Program Committee and organizers taken from the informal proceedings front matter (with affiliations). The website's Program Committee page lists the same members with countries only (and Ileana Streinu separately as Chair; Dongming Wang as France, Xiao-Shan Gao as China). The website's Organizing Committee page lists 7 members without Dan Song.
- 'The informal proceedings contain 14 papers and 3 invited talks. Additional reviewers: Fadoua Ghourabi, Wenping Wang, Menghan Wang, Jeremy Youngquist.'
- The site announced post-proceedings in Springer LNCS/LNAI for invited outstanding contributions; no post-proceedings volume for ADG 2016 is listed on DBLP.
- Invited speakers as on the website's Invited Talks page. In the informal proceedings the names/affiliations read 'Predrag Janičić, Faculty of Mathematics, University of Belgrade, Serbia', 'Dominique Michelucci, LE2I UMR6306, CNRS, Arts et Métiers, Bourgogne Franche-Comté University, Dijon, France' (paper title 'Solving With Or Without Equations') and 'Victor Pambuccian, School of Mathematical and Natural Sciences (MC 2352), Arizona State University - West Campus'.
- Co-located event 'Computer Assisted Theorem Proving for the Education' (June 30, 2016), organized by Julien Narboux, with talks by Tomas Recio (University of Cantabria), Benoit Rognier (edukera.com) and Corneliu Hoffman (University of Birmingham).
- 'Important dates: extended deadline May 2; notification May 23; final submission June 6. Submissions: extended abstracts or full papers (maximum 20 pages).'
- The site is now served at https://adg2016.icube.unistra.fr/ (originally http://icube-web.unistra.fr/adg2016/).
- 'Contact: adg2016@easychair.org.'
