notes:
- The twelfth conference ADG 2018 was held in memory of Wen-tsün Wu, founder of the Chinese school of mathematics mechanization.
- Invited talks are listed as 'Plenary Talk' in the programme; the programme runs September 12-14.
- The conference was organized by Guangxi University for Nationalities and took place at the Landmark Hotel, near Guangxi University for Nationalities and Guangxi University.
- Submission deadline May 25, 2018, extended to June 15, 2018; notification July 15; camera-ready July 31; early registration August 1. Full papers (max 20 pages) or extended abstracts via EasyChair.
- The site announced that accepted full papers would be published as a special issue of some journal or in Springer LNAI/LNCS; no formal post-proceedings volume for ADG 2018 is listed on DBLP.
- The steering committee and bylaws of the ADG conference series were published on the ADG 2018 site (bylaws page). An ADG Business Meeting was chaired by Dongming Wang on September 13.
