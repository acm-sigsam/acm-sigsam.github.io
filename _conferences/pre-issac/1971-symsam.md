notes:
- Dates (23-25 March 1971) and location (Los Angeles, California) from the ACM Digital Library metadata (via Crossref). Joel Moses, who volunteered to organize the conference, later recalled that it took place in July 1971 with about two hundred attendees, and singled out the invited talk on factorization of polynomials by Elwyn Berlekamp (quoted in arXiv:2501.16457); the dates therefore disagree between sources.
- 'Sponsors listed in the ACM metadata: SIGNUM, SIGART, SIAM, SIGPLAN and SIGSAM.'
- The General Chair column of the ISSAC past-chairs table lists Jean E. Sammet, Robert G. Tobey and Joel Moses; the PC chair is given as N/A.
- The ACM Digital Library and DBLP call the meeting SYMSAC '71.
- No committee lists were found online (the ACM Digital Library front matter could not be consulted).
