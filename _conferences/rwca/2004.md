- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Nijmegen, the Netherlands”.
- Ninth edition of a workshop initiated in Strasbourg in 1988 and held every second year since; kept informal, for newcomers to present their work and as a forum aimed at (not restricted to) European researchers.
