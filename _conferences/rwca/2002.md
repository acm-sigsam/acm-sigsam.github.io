notes:
- Eighth edition of the workshop; it is kept informal, to give young researchers and newcomers an opportunity to present their work and to be a regional forum, but is open worldwide. Submissions were not formally refereed.
- 'Programme (as of 8 Feb. 2002): two invited talks, contributed talks in sessions (Geometry; Numeric / Computer Science; Linear Systems and Involutive Bases; Polynomial Systems; Gröbner Bases; Non-commutative Structures), a poster session and a system demo session.'
- The contents of SIGSAM Bulletin / CCA issue 137 (September 2001) list an item 'Rhine Workshop On Computer Algebra' by H. Kredel (page 38).
