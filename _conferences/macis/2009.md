notes:
- Held jointly with the 9th Asian Symposium on Computer Mathematics (ASCM 2009) as "The Joint Conference of ASCM2009 and MACIS2009", supported by the GCOE program "Math-for-industry" of the Graduate School of Mathematics of Kyushu University and the Mathematical Research Center for Industrial Technology of Kyushu University. The programs of ASCM and MACIS were organized independently by their program committees, except the invited talks, which were plenary and shared by both conferences; ASCM and MACIS sessions ran in parallel.
- General Chair, coordinator and local chairs belong to the joint organizing committee of ASCM 2009 and MACIS 2009.
- MACIS 2009 was run in a format where each PC member organized a session on a specific topic; the home page speaks of 9 sessions in three themes but lists the 7 recorded here.
- Selected papers were to be published in special issues of Mathematics in Computer Science (MCS); no proceedings volume.
- The old SIGSAM page gives the website address with a typo (gcoe.math,kyushu-u.ac.jp).
