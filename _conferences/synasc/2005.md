- Organized by the Department of Computer Science, West University of Timisoara; Research Institute for Symbolic Computation, Johannes Kepler University, Linz; and Research Institute e-Austria.

- 'Dates: September 25-29, 2005 on the 2005 site, on synasc.ro Previous editions and in Crossref; the RISC Theorema events list gives September 26 - 30, 2005.'

- Extended abstracts were distributed on a CD at the conference; revised papers were published in post-proceedings by IEEE Computer Society Press. The proceedings DOI is from Crossref.
- The Committees page adds Monica Crubezy, USA to the Program Committee printed in the Call for papers. No tutorials page was found.
