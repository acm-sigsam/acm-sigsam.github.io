notes:
- Organized by the Department of Computer Science, West University, Timisoara; Research Institute for Symbolic Computation, Johannes Kepler University, Linz; and Research Institute e-Austria, Timisoara.
- 'Tracks: Symbolic Computation; Logic and Programming; Artificial Intelligence; Numerical Computing; Distributed Computing; plus the special session Advances in the Theory of Computing.'
- Tutorial 'Symbolic Computation and Theorem Proving for Verified Software', organised by Laura Kovács (Vienna University of Technology), Veronika Pillwein (Research Institute for Symbolic Computation, Linz) and Andrei Voronkov (The University of Manchester), in four sessions.
- Stephen Watt's talk 'Computational Tools for Mathematical Collaboration' was given at a Doctor Honoris Causa Ceremony (final program, Monday 26 September).
- The final program also lists a Session on IBM Involvement in University Research (Florin Manaila - IBM Supercomputers - Energy efficiency with iDataPlex cluster).
- Officers follow the later (August 2011) Committees page. The February 2011 version (and the First Call for Papers) gave the Numerical Computing track chairs as Stefan Maruster and Abdellatif Moudafi (Universite des Antilles-Guyane, Martinique, France) and the Tutorial Chair as Dana Petcu, and did not list special session or workshop chairs (it named Tudor Jebelean as Special Sessions and Workshops Chair).
- The First Call for Papers says the post-proceedings will be published by IEEE Computer Society Press; the Publication page says Conference Publishing Service (CPS). The proceedings DOI is from Crossref.
- Only the Logic and Programming track page with its program committee was found in the archive; no program committee is recorded for the other tracks.
