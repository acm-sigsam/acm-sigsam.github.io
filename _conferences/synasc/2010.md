notes:
- Organized by the Department of Computer Science, West University, Timisoara; Research Institute for Symbolic Computation, Johannes Kepler University, Linz; and Research Institute e-Austria Timisoara.
- 'Tracks: Symbolic Computation; Logic and programming; Artificial Intelligence; Numerical computing; Distributed computing. Special sessions: Advances in the Theory of Computing; Global Computing Models and Technologies; Software Services: Frameworks and Platforms. Workshops: 7th Workshop on Agents for Complex Systems (ACSys); Workshop on Natural Computing and Applications (NCA).'
- Submissions could be research papers (up to 8 pages) or posters (up to 4 pages extended abstract); posters were not published in the post-proceedings.
- The Tutorials page itself only says 'Open invitation' and 'Software Services topics (TBA)'.
- Talk titles are taken from the detailed program (http://wiki.ieat.ro/ieat/Synasc2010/Program, linked from the Program page); the Invited Speakers page lists no titles. The program gives no invited talk by Gerald Schaefer, who is listed on the Invited Speakers page.
- 'The two tutorials (Gogouvitis, Vigano) appear in the WOSS: Workshop on Software Services part of the detailed program, which also lists WoSS invited talks by Huy Tran (TUWIEN), Mircea Trifu (FZI) and Peter Krammer (Slovak Academy of Science).'
- The Program Committee follows the later Committees page (committees.html, captured after February 2011); an earlier version also listed Martin Giese, Norway.
- The Call for papers names Cosmin Bonchis (Institute e-Austria Timisoara, Romania) as Local committee chair.
- Post-proceedings published by Conference Publishing Service (CPS); the site announced 'SYNASC volume is ready!' on Feb 08, 2011. The proceedings DOI is from Crossref.
