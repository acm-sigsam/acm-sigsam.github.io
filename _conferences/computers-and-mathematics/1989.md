- 'DBLP title: "Computers and Mathematics, Third Computers and Mathematics Conference, MIT, Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA, June 13-17, 1989" (editors Erich Kaltofen and Stephen M. Watt).'
- The publisher describes it as the third in a series of conferences devoted to the use of computers in mathematics and the mathematical sciences; the contributed papers were selected from 85 drafts submitted in response to the call for papers.
- The Axiom project's tribute to Richard Jenks says that he, with David Chudnovsky, organized the meetings on Computers and Mathematics held at Stanford in 1986 and MIT in 1989.
