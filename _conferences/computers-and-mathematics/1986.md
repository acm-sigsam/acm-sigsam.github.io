- The proceedings were published under the title Computers in Mathematics; the publisher's description gives the dates (July 29-Aug. 1, 1986, Stanford U.) and says the talks covered the role of computers as a research tool in number theory, analysis, special functions, combinatorics, algebraic geometry, topology and physics. A library record names AAAI and ACM SIGSAM as sponsors. Catalogues disagree on the volume number in the series.
- The Axiom project's tribute to Richard Jenks says that he, with David Chudnovsky, organized the meetings on Computers and Mathematics held at Stanford in 1986 and MIT in 1989.
