- 'There was no separate Calculemus symposium in 2004: IJCAR 2004 was a merger of CADE, FTP, TABLEAUX, FroCoS and CALCULEMUS (IJCAR 2004 home page). The Calculemus meetings list says that in 2002 the CALCULEMUS Interest Group had joined IJCAR for 2004, and the Calculemus 2005 page notes that Calculemus was part of IJCAR 2004.'
- The committees and officers given are those of IJCAR 2004 as a whole; the IJCAR programme committee included Calculemus members such as Alessandro Armando and Wolfgang Windsteiger.
- The site's file and the old SIGSAM list spell the name 'Calulemus 2004'.
