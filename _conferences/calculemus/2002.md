notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Marseille, France”.
- Held in conjunction with AISC 2002 (Artificial Intelligence and Symbolic Computation), Marseille, July 1st-5th, 2002; registration and local arrangements through AISC. The old SIGSAM list and the Calculemus meetings list give the place as 'Marseille Luminy'.
- Both AISC'2002 and CALCULEMUS'2002 were organized by the three Universities of Marseille and the LSIS laboratory.
- Accepted research papers and system descriptions appeared in formal proceedings joint with AISC, published by Springer-Verlag; work in progress and position papers in informal proceedings (Saarland University technical report). The best papers were to appear in a special issue of the Journal of Symbolic Computation.
- The invited speaker (joint event with AISC) is given only as 'to be announced' in all archived copies of the page.
- 'Spelling as on the page: ''Volker Weisspfenning'', ''Univ. de la miditerrannée''.'
