notes:
- Joint workshop of the Calculemus and Types communities, described as the first joint enterprise of the two communities; after five Calculemus workshops restricted to member groups (Rome, Dagstuhl, Linz, Trento, Edinburgh), this one was fully open.
- 'Calculemus meetings list: CALCULEMUS 6 was the first open workshop, co-located with UITP98 (User Interfaces for Theorem Provers).'
- 'No formal proceedings; accepted papers were collected in a web depository, and publication in DMTCS was to be considered during the workshop. Sponsors: EIDMA and RIACA (logos).'
- Lectures in halls K1 and K2 of building RC (Rekencentrum). No programme committee list survives in the archived pages.
