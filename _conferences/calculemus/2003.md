notes:
- Held in conjunction with TPHOLs 2003 (Theorem Proving and Higher Order Logics) and TABLEAUX 2003 (Automated Reasoning with Analytic Tableaux and Related Methods); registration through the Tableaux web page.
- The page describes it as the eleventh symposium in a series which started with three meetings in 1996, two in 1997, and became yearly in 1998.
- 'Emphasis of this year''s conference: heuristics in computer algebra algorithms.'
- The best papers were to be considered for the London Mathematical Society's Journal of Computation and Mathematics, whose Editor-in-Chief joined the Program Committee.
- 'Sponsor: the European Network of Excellence in Computational Logic (CologNet), whose automated reasoning area organized a panel session with Calculemus and Tableaux.'
