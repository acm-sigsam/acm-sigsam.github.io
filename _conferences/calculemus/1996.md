- First of three Calculemus meetings in 1996 (Rome, March 16-19; RISC, Castle Hagenberg, Austria, July 17, Workshop at IMACS ACA; Dagstuhl, Germany, November 18-20, 'Second Meeting of the Math-Assistant Project'), according to the Calculemus meetings list.
- The first five meetings (1996-1997) were internal workshops of the CALCULEMUS consortium, which led to the CALCULEMUS IHP network (Calculemus meetings list).
