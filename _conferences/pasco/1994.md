notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Linz, Austria”.
- Called PASCO'94; the proceedings and DBLP give the place as Hagenberg/Linz, Austria. The call for participation says the symposium takes place in the Castle of Hagenberg, which houses RISC-Linz, about 25 km from Linz.
- 'Sponsors (calls): ACCLAIM (European Union ESPRIT Basic Research Action), CEI-PACT (Central European Initiative on Parallel Computation), BMWF (Austrian Ministry of Science), FWF (Austrian National Science Foundation), ACPC (Austrian Center for Parallel Computation), RISC.'
- SIGSAM's 1995 annual report says the 1994 PASCO meeting was 'in cooperation' with SIGSAM, but due to late approval this was not acknowledged in the conference material (website/reports/95annrpt.html).
- 'Scope: parallel computer algebra, parallel automated deduction, parallel constraint/logic languages, parallel functional languages. Several papers were to appear in a special issue of the Journal of Symbolic Computation.'
- Invited speakers' talk titles are the topic areas given in the calls; the program (program.ps) lists Wang, Hammond and Smolka as 'Invited Tutorial' and Kirchner as 'Invited Talk'. Affiliations are the cities given in the program; the calls give only countries.
- The call for participation calls the local organizers 'Local Arrangements and Technical Support' (Olga Caprotti, Andreas Neubacher, Wolfgang Schreiner, RISC-Linz, Johannes Kepler University) and spells 'B. Fronhofer', 'W. Kuchlin' and 'S. M. Watt' (umlauts lost in the PostScript text); the text call has 'B. Fronhoefer', 'W. Kuechlin', 'S. Watt'.
- Early registration fee ATS 3000, late ATS 3600 (call for participation).
- The program lists 41 contributed papers in 14 sessions over three days, closing with a symposium banquet on 28 September.
- The website field is the symposium's anonymous ftp directory named in the call.
