notes:
- 8th International Workshop on Parallel Symbolic Computation (PASCO).
- 'Name differs: web pages say ''International Workshop on Parallel Symbolic Computation''; the calls (CFP_PASCO17.txt, CFPart_PASCO17.txt) and proceedings title page say ''International Workshop on Parallel and Symbolic Computation''.'
- Co-located with ISSAC 2017 at the Technical University of Kaiserslautern, running just before ISSAC (July 25-28, 2017).
- Talks in Bldg 48, Room 208 (program.HTML).
- 'Officers list taken from the proceedings ''PASCO 2017 Organization'' section (README.txt), which uses ''Communication Chair'' where the web page and calls say ''Publicity chair'', and adds ''Proceedings Editor: Hans-Wolfgang Loidl''.'
- Organising Committee spells 'Jean-Charles Faugère'; Programme Committee list spells 'Jean-Charles Faugere'.
- Proceedings published by ACM through its ICPS programme, ISBN 978-1-4503-5288-8; the PDFs are archived in website/PASCO/2017/papers/.
- 'Sponsor: Maplesoft (README.txt).'
- Moreno Maza's talk is listed under 'Invited Speakers' on the index page but is a 2-hour tutorial (Monday, July 24, 13:30-15:30).
- 'Bientinesi''s talk is titled ''Linnea: Compiling Linear Algebra Expressions to High-Performance Code'' in the program and proceedings.'
- 11 accepted papers listed on papers.html; the proceedings table of contents has 13 items (3 invited/tutorial + 10 contributed); 'A concurrent lock-free algorithm for computing a finite semigroup' (James Mitchell, Julius Jonusas, Markus Pfeiffer) is in the program but not in the proceedings TOC.
- PASCO Business Meeting on Sunday July 23, 16:30-17:30.
- 'Registration fees: Regular 150 Euro, Student 110 Euro; registration via the ISSAC17 web-shop.'
- No photos in the PASCO 2017 folders (only the ACM ICPS logo, a header and a banner image).
- 'Proceedings DOI 10.1145/3115936 (Crossref: event ''PASCO 2017'', Kaiserslautern Germany, sponsor Heriot-Watt University); DBLP lists it as the 6th PASCO (''PASCO@ISSAC 2017''), while the calls say 8th.'
- 'SIGSAM news (4 August 2017): the proceedings are in the ACM Digital Library and contain all papers presented; the event was run in cooperation with SIGSAM.'
- The GAP Forum copy of the call (6 March 2017) gives the same Organising and Programme Committees.
