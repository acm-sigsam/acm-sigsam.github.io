notes:
- 9th International Workshop on Parallel Symbolic Computation (PASCO); the call says 'International Workshop on Parallel and Symbolic Computation'.
- Held in conjunction with the IEEE International Parallel & Distributed Processing Symposium (IPDPS), as a one-day satellite workshop immediately following IPDPS.
- Accepted papers to be included in the IPDPS proceedings; extended abstracts 2-4 pages, selected papers extended to full papers (up to 10 pages).
- The call (CFP_PASCO18.txt) lists Jean-Charles Faugere as '(co-chair)' of the Programme Committee and gives the general chairs as 'Jeremy Johnson, Drexel University USA' and 'Macr Moreno Maza [sic], University of Western Ontario CANADA'; the web page shows no co-chair marks.
- The call's header reads 'CALL FOR PAPERS PASCO 2017' although its content is for PASCO 2018.
- The Programme Committee list is identical to PASCO 2017's.
- index.html and PASCO_2018.html are identical copies.
- The IPDPS 2018 advance program lists 'PASCO Parallel Symbolic Computation CANCELLED' among the Friday 25 May 2018 workshops; the IPDPS 2018 workshops flyer lists PASCO without the mark. No proceedings, program or speakers were found, and DBLP has no PASCO 2018 entry.
