notes:
- SNC'07 and PASCO'2007 were satellite conferences of ISSAC'07 (Waterloo, Canada); SNC 2007 was co-located and both were held immediately prior to ISSAC 2007 at the University of Western Ontario.
- 'Earlier meetings named in the call: PASCO ''94 (Linz, Austria) and PASCO ''97 (Maui, U.S.A.).'
- The call lists the invited speakers as Michael A. Bauer (Univ. of Western Ontario), Matteo Frigo (Cilk Arts), Thierry Gautier (INRIA), Anthony D. Kennedy (Edinburg Univ., jointly with SNC) and Katherine Yelick (Berkeley Univ.); names, affiliations and titles above are from the Invited Talks page.
- 'Tutorials: ''PASCO ''07 - SHARCNET Tutorial Sessions'' on July 26th in room 285 of the North Campus Building.'
- The committees page names the role 'Proceeding Editor' and lists the webmaster; the call names 'Proceedings Editor', 'Publicity' (François Lemaire) and 'Administration' (Meg Borthwick).
- 'Support: The Fields Institute, MITACS, NSERC, The University of Western Ontario (Research Western, Faculty of Science, Dept of Computer Science), ORCCA, Maplesoft, Sharcnet.'
- Crossref files the proceedings under event 'ISSAC07' (London Ontario Canada), sponsors ACM and SIGSAM; DBLP lists it as the 3rd PASCO, with header 'Waterloo, Ontario, Canada' but a proceedings title giving University of Western Ontario, London, Ontario, Canada.
- Proceedings published by ACM and in the ACM Digital Library; papers as extended abstracts (2 pages) or full papers (up to 10 pages); submissions via EasyChair.
