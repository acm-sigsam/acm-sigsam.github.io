notes:
- Name from the proceedings (Crossref) and the ISSAC'97 call ('PASCO'97, the Second International Symposium on Parallel Symbolic Computation'); DBLP calls it the 2nd International Workshop on Parallel Symbolic Computation and gives the place as Kihei, Hawaii, USA.
- Held in federation with ISSAC'97 (July 21-23, 1997) at the same location; the ISSAC'97 call gives PASCO's dates as July 20-23 and ISSAC's venue as the Aston Wailea Resort, Wailea, Hawaii; the SIGSAM CCA archive page and Crossref give PASCO-97 as July 20-22, 1997. The IMACS ACA'97 page says PASCO'97 and ISSAC'97 were held just before ACA at the Aston Wailea Resort.
- Papers on topics of both meetings could be submitted for joint acceptance by the PASCO and ISSAC program committees (ISSAC'97 call).
- 'Sponsors (Crossref): ACM SIGSAM and SIGNUM.'
- 'SIGSAM''s 1998 annual report: ''The ISSAC''97/2nd PASCO conference held in Kihei, (Maui) Hawaii was one of the best-attended meetings in the ISSAC series'' and returned a surplus to SIGSAM and SIGNUM.'
- SIGSAM Bulletin issue 116 contains a PASCO '97 announcement by H. Hong (p. 47); its text is not in the local copy.
- Website as linked from the SIGSAM CCA archive page (http://www.csc.ncsu.edu/pasco96); the Internet Archive has no usable copy of it (404 page / no response). Committees, officers and invited speakers were not found; the ACM DL front matter could not be fetched (HTTP 403).
