notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Bath, United Kingdom”.
- 7th International Workshop on Parallel and Symbolic Computation (CFP_Pasco15.txt).
- Co-located with ISSAC 2015, July 6-9, 2015; the call says it is affiliated with and immediately follows ISSAC 2015, also in Bath.
- The core program was on July 10-11.
- The old SIGSAM site (final call in website/PASCO/2017/ and the PASCO home page) lists no committees; the organization, Program Committee and invited speakers above are from the archived PASCO 2015 website.
- The home page announces the invited speakers as 'Julian Langou (UC Denver), Jean-Louis Roch (INRIA/U. Grenoble), and Allan Steel (U. Sydney)'; the invited-speakers page spells 'Julien Langou'.
- The PASCO 2015 programming challenge was cancelled (challenge page).
- 'Proceedings in the ACM ICPS series and the ACM Digital Library. DBLP and the proceedings title give July 10-12, 2015 (SIGSAM news page: 10-12 July 2015), the conference website July 10-11, 2015; DBLP counts it as the 5th PASCO, the website and SIGSAM news as the 7th.'
- 'Sponsors on the website: Maplesoft, ANR, SIGSAM, HPC@LR; Crossref also lists the University of Bath.'
- The home page says the series has been held roughly every four years since 1988 and lists the earlier meetings CAP'88 (Grenoble), CAP'90 (Ithaca), PASCO'94 (Linz), PASCO'97 (Maui, Hawaii), PASCO'07 (London, Ontario), PASCO'10 (Grenoble).
