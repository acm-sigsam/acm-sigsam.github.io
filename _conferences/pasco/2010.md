notes:
- 'Home page heading: ''Parallel Symbolic Computation 2010, International Workshop''; the text and call use ''International Workshop on Parallel and Symbolic Computation (PASCO)''. Crossref/ACM: ''PASCO ''10: 4th International Workshop on Parallel and Symbolic Computation''.'
- Affiliated with ISSAC 2010 (Munich, Germany) and held immediately prior to it; organized in cooperation with ACM SIGSAM.
- Three-day event with invited presentations and tutorials, contributed research papers and posters; papers as extended abstracts (2 pages) or full papers (up to 10 pages), proceedings published by ACM.
- 'Hosted a Computer Algebra Parallel Programming Contest with two challenges: GCD of univariate polynomials with integer coefficients, and multiplying dense matrices with very large integer coefficients; it was run by the organizing committee (no names given).'
- 'Organization support: SIGSAM, INRIA, CNRS, Grenoble University, University Joseph Fourier, Grenoble Institute of Technology, LIG and LJK, ENSIMAG. Crossref lists sponsors Grenoble University, Grenoble INP / ENSIMAG, INRIA and SIGSAM.'
- The first invited talk is a joint talk by Christophe Monat and Christian Bertin (STMicroelectronics) and Claude-Pierre Jeannerod (INRIA), Grenoble and Lyon, France.
- The Program Committee page spells 'Simon Fraiser University' (sic) and 'Jean-Charles Faugere'.
- Submission deadline April 2, 2010 per the call; the home page says the call closed on 19 April 2010 (extended).
- DBLP lists it as the 4th PASCO.
