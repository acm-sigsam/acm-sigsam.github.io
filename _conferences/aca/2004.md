notes:
- 10th ACA (ACA-2004), July 21-23, 2004, Hilton Hotel, Beaumont, Texas, USA, hosted by Lamar University. No page on math.unm.edu; the conference site http://buchberger.cs.lamar.edu/ACA2004 is gone and was read from the Internet Archive (snapshots of 2004-2006).
- Sessions are the 11 entries of the archived session list (sessionlist.jsp, 'There are 11 sessions'); organizers are the 'Session Chair(s)'/'Session Organizers' named on each session page. Keynote Speakers, Special Invited Speakers and the General Session list only the conference e-mail, so no organizers are recorded.
- 'Title variants: the Groebner session page is headed ''Theory and Applications of Groebner Bases'', the CAGD page ''Computational Aspects of CAGD and Geometric Modeling'', the young session page ''Young Researchers Invitational''. The session list titles are used.'
- Hoon Hong is listed for Elimination Theory as '(Program Committee Member)'. The final program (program.pdf) has no Computer Algebra in Education slot; it has sessions labelled General I and CAGD + General II and an exhibition room.
- 'Keynote and special invited speakers (index page): Gerald Farin, Chandrajit Bajaj, Hoon Hong, Ron Goldman, Shang-ching Chou, Deepak Kapur, Roger Germundsson, Jacques Calmet, Stephen Watt.'
- 'Names, affiliations and titles as in the program PDF (''Keynote & Special Invited Speaker: <name> (<affiliation>) on <title>''). The index page lists the same nine with shorter affiliations: ASU, UT Austin, NCSU, Rice, Wichita, UNM, Wolfram Research, Germany, Canada.'
- The session list has separate 'Keynote Speakers' and 'Special Invited Speakers' entries, but the program labels all nine talks 'Keynote & Special Invited Speaker', so they are not split here.
