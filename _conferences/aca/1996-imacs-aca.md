notes:
- 2nd IMACS ACA, 17-20 July 1996, RISC, Castle Hagenberg, Austria.
- Affiliations of the chairs and Program Committee are countries only, as written on the page.
- Session titles are taken from the session pages (skeletons/*.html); the program page (program-plan.html) splits Automated Theorem Proving and Polynomial Elimination into parts I and II and uses the variants 'Constraint Programming' and 'New Appplications in Mathematica'. The 'Computational Physics' session page is skeletons/Maccone.html.
- Session organizer affiliations are the institution lines on each session page; for Roanes-Lozano, Wang and Wester the page gives only an address or nothing, so no affiliation is recorded.
- No local organizing committee is listed on the conference page.
- No plenary, invited or keynote speakers are mentioned on the conference pages examined (call, program/schedule, session list); the conference used the ACA format in which all talks are given in organized special sessions.
- The 'Scientific Program' (program-plan.html) lists only the opening and parallel sessions; the 'skeletons' pages are session organizers' pages, not invited talks.
