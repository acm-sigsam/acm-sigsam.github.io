notes:
- The thirteenth MEGA conference; the XXV (silver) anniversary of MEGA, celebrated with a special afternoon programme on Wednesday 17th June, including the 70th birthday of Carlo Traverso, organizer of the first MEGA (Castiglioncello, 1990), with brief talks by Carlo Traverso, Alicia Dickenstein, André Galligo, Teo Mora and Bernd Sturmfels.
- Daniel Lazard (Paris 6) gave a special invited talk on the history of MEGA and related groups during the special afternoon; he is listed separately from the eight invited speakers.
- Special Session on Coding Theory and Cryptography (Friday 19th June) with invited speaker Elisa Gorla; no session organizers are named on the site.
- 'Related events listed: Algebraic Statistics 2015; 5th Annual Meeting of the DFG Priority Project SPP 1489; Combinatorial and Experimental Methods in Commutative Algebra and Related Fields.'
