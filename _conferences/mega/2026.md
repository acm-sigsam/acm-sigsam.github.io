notes:
- The nineteenth MEGA conference (EasyChair CFP).
- Organisation page lists 'Chairs' Alessandra Bernardi (Trento) and Kristian Ranestad (Oslo); the EasyChair CFP marks both as Co-chair of the Executive Committee.
- The EasyChair CFP lists an 'Organizing Committee' of Ollie Clarke, Yue Ren and Victoria Schleis; the conference site lists the longer 'Local Organisers' list used here.
- The EasyChair CFP gives Michael Walter's affiliation as Ruhr University Bochum, Germany; the conference site (home and programme pages) gives LMU Munich, Germany, used here.
- Programme page gives Khazhgali Kozhasov's affiliation as Université Côte d'Azur, France.
- 'Submission types: regular papers (extended abstract or full paper) for contributed talks, computation presentations, and posters. Paper deadline 1 December 2025, extended to 9 December 2025.'
- Contributed talks were in parallel thematic sessions (e.g. Computational and Effective Commutative Algebra; Real Projective Geometry and Grassmannian; Algebraic Statistics, Models, Identifiability; Positivity, Real Algebraic Geometry, Polynomial Optimization; Tensor Decompositions and Ranks), plus software presentations and a poster session; no organized special sessions.
- Supported by the UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship Programme (MR/Y003888/1); the London Mathematical Society provided limited funding for UK-based PhD students.
