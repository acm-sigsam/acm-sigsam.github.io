- Committees, plenary talks and affiliations are taken from the Book of Abstracts (the committee and plenary pages of the eventos.ucm.es site are rendered by JavaScript and could not be read, and no Internet Archive copy was found).
- The Book of Abstracts writes the conference chair as 'Theobald Thorsten (Goethe Univ., Frankfurt, Chair of the Conference)' (surname first) and spells Georgia Tech as 'Giorgia Tech. USA'; copied as written. The JSC special issue guest editors are listed on the series page as 'T. Theobald'.

- MEGA 2019 was a registered satellite meeting of ICIAM 2019 (Valencia, July 15-19, 2019). A satellite conference MEGAR was held at the Mathematics Building of UCM, June 21-22, 2019.
- Plenary talks in room S118; contributed talks in two parallel rooms (B15, B16), plus software presentations and a poster session; no organized special sessions.
