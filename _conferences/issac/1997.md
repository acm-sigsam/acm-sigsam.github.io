notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Maui, Hawaii, USA”.
- 'Dates from the DBLP record of the proceedings: "Proceedings of the 1997 International Symposium on Symbolic and Algebraic Computation, ISSAC 1997, Maui, Hawaii, USA, July 21-23, 1997" (ACM 1997).'
- The archived conference site (Drexel) says ISSAC'97 was held in federation with PASCO'97, the Second International Symposium on Parallel Symbolic Computation (July 20-23, 1997), at the Aston Wailea Resort on Maui; IMACS-ACA'97 was held at the same location July 24-26, 1997. 183 registrants from over 20 countries participated; the Maui High Performance Computing Center cooperated in the local arrangements.
- The original site address was http://www.mcs.drexel.edu/ISSAC97/web2/main.html (only Internet Archive copies remain).
- People are from the archived ISSAC'97 contacts page (isnames.html), the draft final program (Conference_Program3.txt) and the tutorials page. Affiliations of officers are the institution lines of their postal addresses.
- The invited speakers page of the archived site says only "This page is under construction!"; the two invited lectures (Monica Lam, Monday July 21; Ton Levelt, Wednesday July 23) are from the draft final program, which gives no affiliations. The Monday lecture fell in the joint ISSAC'97/PASCO'97 schedule.
- 'Three full-day tutorials were given on Sunday, July 20: "Parallel Computer Algebra" (Jean-Louis Roch and Gilles Villard), "Algorithmic geometric and algebraic methods for nonlinear PDE" (Gregory J. Reid and Ian G. Lisle; the program writes "Algorithmic Geometric and Algebraic Methods for Nonlinear PDE") and "Real World" Arithmetic (David Matula).'
