notes:
- Committee page is titled 'Organizing and Program Committees'; affiliations there are given only as countries in parentheses, recorded here as the affiliation.
- Organized by the Key Laboratory of Mathematics Mechanization, Chinese Academy of Sciences.
- Tutorials were held on Sunday, July 24, 2005.
- Paper submission deadline January 21, 2005; notification March 15, 2005; camera-ready copy May 12, 2005.
- 'Related workshops: SNC Workshop July 19-21, 2005 at Xi-An; AMC Workshop July 23-24, 2005, Beijing; IAMC Workshop July 24, 2005, Beijing. Satellite workshops listed in the call: Internet Accessible Mathematical Computation; Symbolic-Numeric Computation; Algebraic Methods in Cryptography.'
- Sponsored by ACM, with financial support from National Natural Science Foundation of China, Maplesoft, Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, Institute of Systems Science, Key Laboratory of Mathematics Mechanization, and INRIA (Institut National De Recherche En Informatique Et En Automatique).
- Accommodation at the Beijing Friendship Hotel and the Guest House of Beijing Institute of Technology.
- Website and proceedings links are taken from the ISSAC 2007 'previous ISSACs' page and the ISSAC 2008 links page (homepage also stated in the 2005 call for papers).
- 'Photos: the 57 conference photos come from the gallery page website/issac/2005/issac05_photo/issac05photo.html (each also has a thumbnail in issac05_photo/thumbnails/); the gallery shows only file names as captions, so captions are null. The gallery page title reads ''ISSAC 2005 25-27 July, Beijing China'', which differs from the July 24-27 dates elsewhere. Beijing tourist-attraction pictures (images/200x150, images/bi-pic), maps and the conference poster image are not listed as photos.'
- Great Wall picture (images/picture/greatwall.jpg) illustrates the Post-ISSAC excursion to the Great Wall on July 28, 2005 (socialevents page).
