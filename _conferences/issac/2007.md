notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Waterloo, Ontario, Canada”.
- Committee page is titled 'Organizing Committee'; affiliations there are countries only.
- The Software Exhibitions chair's name (Hirokazu Anai) is written by an e-mail obfuscation script (and an HTML comment) on the committee page, so plain-text conversion shows only 'Japan'.
- The General Information page says the organizers and ISSAC steering committee members are listed on the committee page, but no steering committee appears there.
- Tutorials and reception July 29, 2007; conference July 30 - August 1, 2007. Tutorials held Sunday, July 29.
- Paper deadline January 17, 2007, extended to January 24, 2007; notification March 28, 2007; camera-ready deadline May 17, 2007 (originally April 27).
- Invited talk titles were not given ('Titles and abstracts will be forthcoming').
- A special issue of the Journal of Symbolic Computation was planned for revised versions of ISSAC 2007 papers; guest editors Carlos D'Andrea and Bernard Mourrain.
- 'Related conferences: ACA 2007 (July 19-22, 2007), SNC''07 (July 25-27, 2007), PASCO''07 (July 27-28, 2007).'
- Officially sponsored by ACM, with financial sponsorship by University of Waterloo, Waterloo Symbolic Computation Group, MITACS and Maplesoft.
- Described as the 32nd ISSAC meeting.
- Website URL is taken from the ISSAC 2008 links page.
- 'Photos: only the home-page collage image (images/Collage.png, alt text ''[Collage Picture]'') is a photograph; it shows campus/local scenes. Logos and the conference poster are not listed.'
