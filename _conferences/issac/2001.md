notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “London, Ontario, Canada”.
- 'Locally hosted by the University of Western Ontario and the Ontario Research Centre for Computer Algebra (ORCCA). Conference hall: Conron Hall, University College.'
- Tutorials on Sunday, July 22nd, 2001 at the University of Western Ontario (Krandick 08:30-11:30; Giesbrecht & Storjohann 12:45-15:45; Dooley 16:00-17:30). Tutorial pages describe Krandick as professor at Drexel University, Giesbrecht at the University of Western Ontario / ORCCA, Storjohann at ORCCA, Dooley at IBM T. J. Watson Research Center.
- Proceedings Editor is listed as a single-person entry on the committee page; it is recorded under officers only.
- 'An IAMC 2001 Workshop was organised (committee listed). Neighbouring events: CoCoA meeting and computer algebra school at Queen''s University, Kingston (July 16-21); Workshop on Future Directions for Categorical Programming Languages (Aldor emphasis) at UWO on July 26, 2001; International Conference on Dynamics of Continuous, Discrete and Impulsive Systems, London Ontario, July 27-31, 2001.'
- A best student author award was offered. The menu also links a JSC Special Issue.
- The Call for Papers lists the same 16-member Program Committee (with Gilles Villard at Laboratoire LIP, Ecole Normale Superieure de Lyon) and Bernard Mourrain (INRIA Sophia Antipolis) as Proceedings Editor.
- 'Date disagreement within the Call for Papers: the Important Dates list notification March 19, 2001 and camera-ready April 5, 2001, while the Notification paragraph says on or before March 12, 2001 and April 2, 2001.'
- 'Location ''London, Ontario, Canada'' is taken from the committee page affiliations and from the ISSAC 2000 site (issac_org.html: ''ISSAC 2001, July 22-25, 2001, London, Ontario, Canada''); the 2001 home page names only the University of Western Ontario.'
- Photo stationparkinn.jpg appears without alt text on the accommodation page, next to the Station Park Inn hotel entry. The conference poster (images/issacposter.jpg/.pdf) and the direction map are not counted as photos.
- 'The menubar also links an external posters page: http://mast.queensu.ca/~bigatti/ISSACposters/.'
