notes:
- 'The home page of the previous site cites “ISSAC ''23: Proceedings of the 2023 on International Symposium on Symbolic and Algebraic Computation, July 2023”.'
- The 48th meeting in the series. The home page does not state the dates; the program page runs from Monday July 24th to Thursday July 27th, 2023, and the DBLP record of the proceedings gives July 24-27, 2023.
- The conference took place at the Scandic Ishavshotel in the center of Tromsø (local information page).
- 'Satellite workshops listed on the site: one held June 26-30, 2023 in Bergen, Norway, and the 8th International Workshop on Satisfiability Checking and Symbolic Computation, July 28, 2023, in Tromsø.'
- The home page lists the invited talk of Rekha R. Thomas as "Classical Invariant Theory in Computer Vision"; the Invited Talks page gives "Two Views of P3", which is recorded.
