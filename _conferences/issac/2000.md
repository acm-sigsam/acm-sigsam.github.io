notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “St Andrews, Fife, Scotland”.
- Tutorials on Sunday August 6; conference August 7-9, 2000.
- Held concurrently with CALCULEMUS-2000, the Eighth Symposium on the Integration of Symbolic Computation and Mechanized Reasoning, August 6-7; the invited talk by H. Barendregt and A. M. Cohen (joint talk) was held jointly with CALCULEMUS.
- Sponsored by ACM-SIGSAM. Supported by the Edinburgh Mathematical Society (EMS), the London Mathematical Society, Numerical Algorithms Group Ltd., Texas Instruments Inc. and Waterloo Maple Inc.
- Tutorial 3 (Rayes) was sponsored by Texas Instruments and free of charge.
- James Davenport gave an after-dinner speech (Maple code on computer algebra and golf supporting it is linked from the home page).
- Program lists an ISSAC Business Meeting and a SIGSAM Business Meeting on 8 August, and a banquet that evening.
- The ISSAC Organization page states that ISSAC 2000 is the 25th meeting of a series dating back to 1966, and that bylaws adopted at the Montreal meeting state how the meetings are run by the ISSAC Steering Committee (no members listed).
- 'Minor differences between pages: the home page gives ''Arjeh Cohen'' and ''University of St. Andrews'' (for Steve Linton), the Call for Posters gives ''Arjeh M. Cohen'' (RIACA, Department of Mathematics and Computing Science, Eindhoven University of Technology); the committee page was used.'
- The proceedings link is the series-wide list of ISSAC proceedings given on the ISSAC Organization page, not a page specific to ISSAC 2000.
- Photo Images/NHroom.gif is linked from the accommodation page as a room in New Hall, the recommended student residence.
- The accommodation page says the main conference events take place in the Physical Sciences building of the University.
