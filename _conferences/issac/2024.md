notes:
- Dates and place from an announcement on the home page of the previous site, written before the meeting (hidden in the page source).

- The 49th meeting in the series. The home page does not state the dates; the program page runs from Tuesday July 16th to Friday, July 19th, 2024, and the DBLP record of the proceedings gives "Raleigh, NC, USA, July 16-19, 2024".
