notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Lille-Villeneuve d'Ascq, France”.
- Officers are the role-holders of the 'Conference Committee' table on committee.html; they are listed both as committee members (with roles) and in 'officers'.
- Tutorials took place on Sunday, July 7th, at the Macc, Université des Sciences et Technologies de Lille, Lille-Villeneuve d'Ascq, France.
- 'Satellite conference: Internet Accessible Mathematical Computation (IAMC Workshop, July 7 per the program).'
- 'Related events: a French-language week-end initiation workshop on computer algebra (July 6-7), hosted by ISSAC; Second Workshop on Categorical Programming Languages with an Emphasis on Aldor, Thursday July 11, 2002, MACC, University of Lille I (satellite event).'
- 'Sponsors/supporters shown on the home page: ACM; Laboratoire d''Informatique de Lille; Université des Sciences et Technologies de Lille; Centre de calcul formel Médicis; Conseil régional du Nord Pas-de-Calais; Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique; Mairie de Villeneuve d''Ascq; Waterloo Maple; Texas Instruments.'
- A Best Student Award is given to the best student author (callpaper.html).
- 'Paper deadlines (extended): submissions January 14, 2002; notification March 7, 2002; camera-ready March 22, 2002.'
- 'Name variants: the PC chair appears as ''Luis Miguel Pardo Vasallo'' (Conference Committee), ''Luis M. Pardo'' (Program Committee list) and ''Luis Miguel Pardo'' (callpaper.html); ''Mark van Hoeij'' (committee.html) vs ''Mark Van Hoeij'' (callpaper.html).'
- Tutorial 2 title is 'combinatorical problems' on tutorials.html and program.tex, 'combinatorial problems' on tutorial2.html and program.html.
- 'Tutorial times differ: tutorials.html gives 8h30-11h30, 12h30-15h30, 15h45-18h45; program.html/program.tex give 8h30-11h45 (with break), 12h45-15h45, 16h00-19h00.'
- Invited speaker affiliations are not given on the site.
- Poster by François Lemaire and Sylvain Neut.
- The website URL http://www.lifl.fr/issac2002 is stated in calldemo.pdf, which places the meeting at 'University of Lille I, France, July 7-10, 2002'.
- 'Photos: vucongres.jpg and amphi.jpeg (accommodation.html) show the MACC conference facilities; the others are local-information photos on localinfo.html (Grand Place, the giants Gayant and Marie-Cagenon, fairs, Flemish dishes), not pictures of the meeting itself. gayant.gif, marie-cagenon.gif, geant20.gif, braderie.jpg and psn00008.jpg have no alt text. Excluded: logos, the conference poster (bigpict/affiche.jpg, pictures/poster*.jpg/.tif) and the subway map (pictures/metro.gif).'
