notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA”.
- Affiliations in organization.html are countries only; Poster Committee affiliations are taken from call_for_posters.html, where that committee is headed 'Program Committee'.
- Tutorials on Sunday, August 3 (Drexel University, Pearlstein Building, Room 101); talks August 4-6 in Behrakis Grand Hall; welcome reception at the Main Building, Westphal Picture Gallery; banquet at The Warwick, Grand Ballroom (advance-program.html).
- 'August 7, 2003: IAMC workshop, CHEMCAL workshop and satellite workshops; satellite workshops selected by the general chair in consultation with the steering committee (satellite.html). No steering committee membership is listed.'
- Sponsored by ACM, with support from the College of Engineering, Department of Computer Science and Student Union Services of Drexel University, and Maplesoft.
- 'Paper deadlines: submission January 19, 2003; notification March 25, 2003; camera-ready May 12, 2003 (call_for_papers.html also mentions a final copy required by April 14, 2003).'
- Exhibits chair email is aszanto@math.ncsu.edu on organization.html but aszanto@ncsu.edu on call_for_software.html.
- 'Invited talk chairs: Werner Krandick (Barnett), Evelyne Hubert (Kaltofen), Karin Gatermann (Laubenbacher). Banquet speech by Stephen Watt.'
- Site pages maintained by William Turner and jointly designed by Manfred Minimair and William Turner.
- 'website/issac/2003-old/ contains only a posters list (index.html, posters.html), poster-howto.html and poster files; its 15-poster list is identical to website/issac/2003/posters.html (only page titles differ: ''ISSAC 2003 Posters'' vs ''Accepted Posters''). No disagreement on committees, dates or speakers.'
- Invited speaker affiliations are not stated on invited-talks.html (only e-mail addresses).
- Calls are those listed under 'Calls' in menu.html (Research Papers, Posters, Software Exhibitions, IAMC workshop, CHEMCAL workshop, Satellite workshops). The URL given for the papers/posters/software/satellite calls is the conference URL printed on those pages; chemcal.html is a workshop programme rather than a call text.
- 'Photos (photos.html, ''Photo Gallery''): Reception - Drexel Main Building, Great Court, Nike statue; Tutorials - Korman Center (in the background); Dormitory - GirlInTowers.jpg, NorthInterior.jpg (no captions). PHILADEL.jpg is shown on main.html without caption. Each photo exists in full size and as a thumbnail under Images/Thumbs/. Tutorial speaker photos are linked from tutorials.html (''Photos of Martin Peternell and Helmut Pottmann'', ''Photo of Tudor Jebelean'', ''Photo of Gavin Brown'').'
- pictures.html ('Conference Pictures') says Tony Shaska has many pictures from the ISSAC banquet on his web page (http://www.uidaho.edu/~tshaska/pic/ISSAC); none are archived here.
- 'Excluded images: page-heading banners (Images/*.jpg with alt text), logos, conference poster (posterISSAC*), proceedings cover (newcover*.jpg), and posters/Kotsireas.jpg (a poster file, present in both 2003 and 2003-old).'
