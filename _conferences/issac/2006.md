notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Genova, Italy”.
- Hosted by DIMA, University of Genova. The 'Previous ISSACs' list on the ISSAC 2007 site gives the place as 'Genoa, Italy'.
- Tutorials on July 9, 2006; main conference July 10-12, 2006 (room 508 at DIMA for plenary sessions).
- Held just after CALCULEMUS-2006, the 13th Symposium on the Integration of Symbolic Computation and Mechanized Reasoning, Genova, 7-8 July; Café Conference (Nice) on 13 July 2006.
- Paper submission deadline January 18, 2006; notification March 17, 2006; camera-ready April 28, 2006. Submission via EasyChair.
- The 2006 Richard D. Jenks Prize for Excellence in Software Engineering Applied to Computer Algebra was to be awarded at ISSAC 2006.
- Committee page gives the Poster & Software Exhibitions Committee without affiliations and lists Pascal Giorgi as 'Soft. chair'; the call for posters/software page gives affiliations and 'Soft. Ex. chair' (used here).
- No steering committee is listed on the 2006 site.
- Proceedings link is taken from the ISSAC 2007 'previous ISSACs' page.
- No conference photos are archived in the 2006 folder; the home page links to an external photo page http://issac2006.dima.unige.it/photos/ which is not archived. Images of Genova (lanterna, fountain), maps, floor plans and the conference poster are not listed as photos.
