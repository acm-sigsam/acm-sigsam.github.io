notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Vancouver BC CANADA”.
- 'Dates, place and venue from the preliminary announcement on the archived conference site (http://www.cecm.sfu.ca/ISSAC99): "July 28-31, 1999, Simon Fraser University, Vancouver BC CANADA"; the symposium was to be held at the university''s Harbour Centre campus in the centre of Vancouver. Sponsored by ACM-SIGSAM and ACM-SIGNUM.'
- The DBLP record of the proceedings gives July 29-31, 1999 ("ISSAC '99, Vancouver, B.C., Canada, July 29-31, 1999").
- People are from the archived ISSAC'99 pages (Organizing Committee, Poster Sessions, Tutorials, Advance Program). Affiliations of officers are the institution lines of their postal addresses.
- The Organizing Committee page writes the program committee member "J. McKay Concordia" with affiliation "U.Concordia, Canada"; recorded here as "J. McKay".
- The three invited talks (Thursday 29, Friday 30 and Saturday 31 July) are from the Advance Program, which gives no affiliations. The two tutorials were given on Wednesday 28 July (Tutorial 1, 10:00-12:30, Corless and Shampine; Tutorial 2, 14:30-17:00, Chyzak).
- The pre-conference IAMC Workshop (Internet Accessible Mathematical Computation) on Wednesday 28 July was organized by Paul Wang and Norbert Kajler, with an invited speaker, Henri Casanova (UCSD).
