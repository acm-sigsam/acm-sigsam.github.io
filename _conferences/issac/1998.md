notes:
- Sponsored by GI, in cooperation with ACM-SIGSAM and ACM-SIGNUM.
- The committee page is titled 'Organizing Committee'; all roles above are listed there. The Program Committee members at large are listed with e-mail addresses only, no affiliations.
- Three 3-hour tutorial courses were held on August 12, 1998, preceding the symposium (Duval 7:30-10:30, Stetter 11:00-14:00, Bronstein 15:00-18:00).
- A mini-symposium 'The Future of Mathematical Problem-Solving Environments', organised by NAG Ltd. and Waterloo Maple Inc. (contact Mike Dewar), was held on August 12.
- Program also lists a Business Meeting (Aug 13), a banquet in Warnemünde (Aug 13), poster sessions, and a meeting of the Computer Algebra Benchmarking Initiative (Aug 14).
- Participants could attend IMACS ACA '98 in Prague beforehand and ICM'98 (August 18-27, Berlin) afterwards.
- The Call for Posters says poster committee members would be announced later and names only the Chair and Vice-Chair. The Poster Chair's e-mail is glabahn@daisy.waterloo.ca on the committee page but glabahn@daisy.uwaterloo.ca on the Call for Posters.
- 'Important dates: submissions 30 Jan 1998; notification 24 Mar 1998; camera-ready 28 Apr 1998; poster abstracts 16 Jun 1998.'
- Tutorials were published as an 'Announcement of Tutorials' (tutorial.html), not as a call; no archived Call for Papers page exists, the call text is part of main.html.
