notes:
- In conjunction with the Federated Computing Research Conference (FCRC 2011)
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “San Jose, USA”.
- The 36th meeting in the series, held as part of the ACM Federated Computing Research Conference (FCRC) in San Jose. The site gives tutorials on Wednesday, June 8, and the main program on June 9–11; it does not give one overall date range or the venue.
- ISSAC 2011 was affiliated with the 2011 International Workshop on Symbolic Numeric Computation (SNC 2011), June 7–9, 2011, another member of the ACM FCRC. The 4th Richard D. Jenks Memorial Prize was awarded at ISSAC/SNC 2011 in San Jose on June 9, 2011.
- The DBLP record of the proceedings gives June 7-11, 2011 ("ISSAC 2011 (co-located with FCRC 2011), San Jose, CA, USA, June 7-11, 2011").
- Three tutorials were held on Wednesday, June 8; the invited plenary talks were in the main program on June 9–11.
- E-mail addresses that the committees page prints next to two names are left out.
