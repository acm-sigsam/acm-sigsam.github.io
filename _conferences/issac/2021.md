notes:
- Dates and place from an announcement on the Upcoming Conferences page of the previous site, written before the meeting (hidden in the page source).
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Saint Petersburg, Russia (online and in-person)”.
- The 46th meeting in the series, run in a hybrid mode (largest attendance online, in-person attendance possible), with working times from 14:00 to 20:00 Saint Petersburg time. Tutorials Monday-Tuesday, 19-20 July, 2021; conference Tuesday-Friday, 20-23 July, 2021.
- A pre-conference workshop in honor of Vladimir Gerdt took place on Sunday, 18 July, 2021. The DBLP record of the proceedings gives "Virtual Event, Russia, July 18-23, 2021".
- The tutorial "Massively parallel computations in algebraic geometry" was given jointly by Janko Böhm and Anne Frühbis-Krüger.
