notes:
- 'CICM 2012 consisted of five tracks: Track A: Artificial Intelligence and Symbolic Computation (AISC), Track B: Calculemus, Track C: Digital Mathematical Libraries (DML), Track D: Mathematical Knowledge Management (MKM), Track E: Systems & Projects.'
- The home page says the conference was organized by Serge Autexier (DFKI) and Michael Kohlhase (JUB) and that the overall programme was organized by the General Program Chair Johan Jeuring.
- 'Header dates are 8.-13. July 2012; the call for papers gives ''Conference: 9-13 July 2012'' (the MIR workshop was on Sunday 8 July).'
- 'Co-located workshops: MathUI 2012, MIR 2012, OpenMath 2012, UITP 2012, THedu 2012, plus a Doctoral Programme (application deadline 16 May 2012).'
- Michael Trott's talk 'Mathematical Search' ([M12], Sunday) is listed under 'Invited Talks' on the talks page; its M-number places it in the MIR workshop programme. No affiliation is given.
- Special Address by Stephen Wolfram (Wednesday, 19:00-20:00, Campus Center/IRC, Library, 5th Floor).
- 'Panel discussion ''Towards World DML: Are We on the Right Track?'' with Jiří Rákosník, Patrick Ion, Alan Sexton, Wolfram Sperber.'
- 'Venue: Lecture Hall of the building ''Research III'', Campus Ring 8, Jacobs University; workshops and doctoral programme on 11 July in West Hall.'
- Joris van der Hoeven's affiliation is written '&Eacute Polytechnique, France' in the page source (rendered as 'É Polytechnique').
- The MKM track chair Makarius Wenzel is listed as 'University of Paris-South' in the call for papers and as 'Université Paris-Sud 11, France' in the MKM programme committee list (no role marked there; the call names him chair).
- Proceedings volume number (7362) and DOI verified from the DBLP conf/mkm index (archived copy) and Crossref; the call says the proceedings appear in Springer's Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence (LNAI).
- 'Next meeting announced on the page: CICM 2013 in Bath, UK, organized by James Davenport.'
