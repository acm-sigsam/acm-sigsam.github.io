notes:
- CICM 2008 combined the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Symbolic Computation (AISC), the Symposium on the Integration of Symbolic and Mechanized Reasonings (Calculemus) and the International Conference on Mathematical Knowledge Management (MKM), with workshops and a doctoral programme.
- 'Constituent conferences: MKM (6th International Conference on Mathematical Knowledge Management, 28 July - 30 July), Calculemus (15th Symposium on the Integration of Symbolic and Mechanized Reasoning, 30 July - 1 August), AISC (9th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Symbolic Computation, 31 July - 1 August). The conferences page numbers MKM 2008 as the 6th conference, while the proceedings title (DBLP/Springer) calls it the 7th.'
- The archived pages give no overall date range; date_text is taken from the proceedings title recorded in DBLP/Crossref ('Birmingham, UK, July 28 - August 1, 2008'). Workshops started on 27 July.
- 'Workshops: ESARM (Empirically Successful Automated Reasoning for Mathematics, 27 July), PLMMS (Programming Languages for Mechanized Mathematics Systems, 29 July), ARW (15th Workshop on Automated Reasoning: Bridging the Gap between Theory and Practice, 30-31 July), MathUI (Mathematical User-Interfaces Workshop, 27 July), DML (Towards Digital Mathematics Library, 27 July).'
- 'Invited speakers by event: MKM: Alan Bundy, Thierry Bouche; Calculemus: Annie Cuyt (on an integrated environment for reliable numerical computation), Thierry Coquand (on type theory); AISC: Jochen Pfalzgraf, Steve Linton. No talk titles are given.'
- 'Venue: School of Medicine, University of Birmingham, Vincent Drive, Edgbaston, Birmingham B15 2TT.'
- The live site is gone; all pages were read from Internet Archive copies (2009-2010 captures).
- Proceedings volume number (5144) and DOI verified from the DBLP conf/mkm index (archived copy) and Crossref; the site links to the Springer book page for ISBN 978-3-540-85109-7.
