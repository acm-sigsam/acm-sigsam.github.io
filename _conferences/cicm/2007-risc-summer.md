notes:
- The 2007 RISC Summer in Linz, Austria was not called CICM, but had the same participating conferences.
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Hagenberg, Austria”.
- The RISC Summer 2007 is a sequence of international scientific events organized by the Research Institute for Symbolic Computation (RISC) in the summer of 2007. It is listed as the 2007 meeting on the CICM series page; it was not itself called CICM.
- 'Events: Axiom Workshop 2007 (June 14-16, Hagenberg); CoCoA School (June 18-22, Hagenberg); SCIEnce Training Event (June 24-July 8, Hagenberg); MEGA 2007 (June 24-30, Strobl); OpenMath/MathML/JEM 2007 (June 25-26, Hagenberg); WING 2007 (June 25-26, Hagenberg); MathUI 2007 (June 27, Hagenberg); Calculemus 2007, 14th Symposium on the Integration of Symbolic Computation and Mechanized Reasoning (June 27-29, Hagenberg); MKM 2007, 6th International Conference on Mathematical Knowledge Management (June 28-30, Hagenberg); PLMMS 2007 (June 29-30, Hagenberg); AB 2007, Second International Conference on Algebraic Biology (July 2-4, Hagenberg); ISPDC 2007 (July 5-8, Hagenberg).'
- date_start/date_end span the listed events (June 14 - July 8, 2007); the page gives no single date range. Calculemus 2007 and MKM 2007 were held jointly, June 27-30, 2007 in Hagenberg.
- The RISC Summer page names no organizers apart from the page maintainer (Wolfgang Schreiner). Officers and committees here are those of the joint Calculemus 2007 and MKM 2007 meetings (the CICM precursors).
- 'Invited talks of Calculemus and MKM were scheduled as joint sessions (Calculemus program page): John Harrison, Peter Paule, Neil Sloane, Tom Hales, Peter Murray-Rust. Titles and affiliations are given only for the two MKM invited speakers (MKM 2007 home page, which writes ''Peter Murray Rust'').'
- Calculemus 2007 sponsors include RISC, JKU, Linzer Hochschulfonds and uni software plus, among others shown as logos.
- Proceedings volume number (4573) stated on the Calculemus 2007 and MKM 2007 pages; DOI verified from Crossref and the DBLP conf/mkm index (archived copy).
