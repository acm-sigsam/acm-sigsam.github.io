notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Bertinoro, Forli (ITALY)”.
- 'Tracks: Track A: MKM, Track B: Calculemus, Track C: Systems & Projects. After colocating as CICM, MKM and Calculemus formally joined for CICM 2011 (call for papers).'
- The Systems and Projects programme committee also includes the 'members of the MKM and Calculemus programme committees'.
- The combined pc.html page lists the MKM and Calculemus committees without James Davenport; the individual track pages (trackA.html, trackB.html) list him as Chair of each, alongside the track chair. The track pages are used here.
- The organizing committee consists of the local organizers, the program committee chairs of the constituent meetings, and past organizers as advisors (organisers.html). Affiliations are written in mixed formats on that page, e.g. 'Andrea Asperti, University of Bologna (Italy)'; they are recorded with the country in parentheses as written.
- 'Co-located workshops: DML 2011 - 4th International Workshop "Towards a Digital Mathematics Library"; OMWS 2011 - 24rd International OpenMath Workshop.'
- CICM 2011 gratefully acknowledges the support of the Department of Computer Science of the University of Bologna.
- Work-in-progress submissions were also invited (proceedings as a technical report).
- Proceedings volume number (6824) and DOI verified from the DBLP conf/mkm index (archived copy) and Crossref; the call says the proceedings appear in Springer's Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence (LNAI).
