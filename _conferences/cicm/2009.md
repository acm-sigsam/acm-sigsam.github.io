notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Grand Bend, Ontario (CANADA)”.
- 'CICM 2009 includes two international conferences: 16th Symposium on the Integration of Symbolic and Mechanized Reasoning - Calculemus, 6-7 July; 8th International Conference on Mathematical Knowledge Management - MKM, 10-12 July.'
- 'Workshops: 2nd Workshop on Compact Computer Algebra - CCA, 10 July; 2nd Workshop on Towards a Digital Mathematics Library - DML, 8-9 July; 4th Workshop on Mathematical User Interfaces - MathUI, 6 July; 22nd Workshop on OpenMath, 9 July; 3rd Workshop on Pen-Based Mathematical Computation - PenMath, 8-9 July; W3C Math Working Group Meeting, 7 July. The W3C MMI (InkMMI) workshop was cancelled.'
- The organizing committee consists of the local organizers, the program committee chairs of the constituent meetings, and past organizers as advisors; constituent events each have their own program committees. Affiliations on that page are written 'Name, Institution (Country)'.
- 'Invited (plenary) speakers: the home page gives shorter affiliations (e.g. ''Rob Arthan (Lemma 1 & Queen Mary, University of London, UK)'', ''Patrick Ion (Math Reviews, USA)'', ''Marko Panic (Microsoft Development, Serbia)'', ''David Ruddy (Cornell University, USA)''); the invited.html page''s forms are used here.'
- Names copied as written on the MKM committee page, including 'Claudio Sacerdoti Cohen', 'Jakobs U' and 'Rabdoub U'.
