notes:
- A satellite workshop of ISSAC 2005.
- The official site is offline; its pages were read from Internet Archive copies (org.html 2004-10-13, inv.html 2004-10-27, pub.html 2004-10-27, ven.html 2004-10-27, Welcome.html 2004-11-06, pro.html 2006-09-26).
- The CAM-Net Digest call also lists Bruno Buchberger (Austria) as 'Open Session Speaker'; he does not appear on the archived invited-speakers page.
- The banquet speaker (program, July 20) was Stephen Watt.
- The welcome page presents SNC 2005 as resuming earlier meetings such as SNAP '96 in Nice, France and the SNAP minisymposium at SIAM '98.
- Informal proceedings with accepted extended abstracts and full papers were to be distributed at the workshop; a formally refereed proceedings volume was planned afterwards (publication page). The Birkhäuser volume edited by the two PC co-chairs is listed as the proceedings on the basis of search-engine summaries of its Springer page (described as inspired by SNC 2005); the Springer page itself could not be read directly (login redirect).
- Submissions went to the PC co-chairs; first deadline April 10, 2005, extended to April 20, 2005.
- 'Listed as ''SNC 2005, Xi''an, China'' in the SNC section (''SNC: International Workshop on Symbolic-Numeric Computing'') of the SIGSAM Conference Series page.'
