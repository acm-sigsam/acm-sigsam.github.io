notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “Sophia Antipolis, France”.
- The meeting was organized by SAFIR, a common project between UNSA, INRIA, and CNRS, at Sophia Antipolis (between Cannes and Nice).
- The workshop was supported in part by the GDR AMI and FRISCO.
- No program committee or invited speakers are listed; the five people under 'Contacts' are recorded as a committee named 'Contacts'.
- The program (prog.txt) lists nine sessions with tentative chairpersons (Watt, Kaltofen, Gianni, Corless, Geddes, Karmarkar, Mourrain, Emiris, Galligo), each talk 25 min plus 5 min questions; an 18h30 boat trip to the Iles de Lerins is listed.
- The page also links photos ('SNAP-shots', thanks to Erich Kaltofen), the list of participants, travel information and a registration form.
- Later SNC calls refer to this meeting as 'the SNAP 96 Workshop, held in Sophia Antipolis, France'; the SNC 2005 site calls it 'SNAP '96 in Nice, France'.
- 'Listed as ''SNAP 1996, INRIA Sophia Antipolis, France'' in the SNC section (''SNC: International Workshop on Symbolic-Numeric Computing'') of the SIGSAM Conference Series page.'
