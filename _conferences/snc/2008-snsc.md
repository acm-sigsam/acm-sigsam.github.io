notes:
- SNSC'08 took place as part of a concluding event of the Special Research Program (SFB) F013 "Numerical and Symbolic Scientific Computing"; the results of 10 years of SFB research were presented.
- SNSC'08 was part of the RISC Summer 2008 series of conferences at Hagenberg.
- The SFB page says the SFB had already organized SNSC'99 (1999, Hagenberg), SNSC'01 (2001, Hagenberg) and NSSC'03 (Strobl/Wolfgangsee, 2003; 'numerical' and 'symbolic' switched in the title because of a stronger emphasis on numerics).
- 'Planned structure: invited talks (45 min), contributed talks (20 min), ''SFB Talks'' (20 min), and a panel discussion on Thursday afternoon (July 24).'
- The archived SFB page (April 2008) lists four invited speakers ('not completed yet'), with affiliations 'Max-Planck Institut, Germany' (Hackbusch) and 'Technical University of Braunschweig, Germany' (Matthies); the April 2008 announcement lists six.
