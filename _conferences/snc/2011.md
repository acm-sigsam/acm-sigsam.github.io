notes:
- The meeting's own site gives the location as “San Jose, California”.
- Held as a member of the ACM Federated Computing Research Conference (FCRC); affiliated with ISSAC 2011 (June 8-11, 2011, also part of FCRC).
- The invited-speakers page gives 'Laureate Professor Jonathan Borwein', 'Professor James Demmel' and 'Professor Stephen Watt'. Watt's talk title is from the PDF program (Thursday, June 9, 2011); the HTML program lists only June 7-8.
- The 4th Richard D. Jenks Memorial Prize was to be awarded at ISSAC/SNC 2011 at FCRC in San Jose on June 9, 2011.
- A special issue of Theoretical Computer Science was planned for selected papers.
- 'Submission deadline: March 15, 2011 in the call for papers; March 23, 2011 on the home page. Camera-ready: April 30, 2011 (call) vs May 6, 2011 (home page).'
- Sponsor logos on the home page link to Maplesoft, Wilfrid Laurier University, NAG, ORCCA and ACM.
- The venue within San Jose is not stated; hotel information was on the FCRC page.
- 'Listed as ''SNC 2011, San Jose, California, USA'' in the SNC section (''SNC: International Workshop on Symbolic-Numeric Computing'') of the SIGSAM Conference Series page.'
