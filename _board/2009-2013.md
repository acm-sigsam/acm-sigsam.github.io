notes:
- The AGM/2009 and AGM/2010 slides give the elected term as July 2009 - June 2011 / 2009-2011. The 2010 and 2011 reports say the officers asked to remain for a second term, which was being considered by the membership; the AGM/2011 slides say 'Elected (2009-2013)', AGM/2013 'Outgoing (2009-2013)', and the 2012 report says they complete their second 2-year term at the end of FY 2013. News/index.html says Agnes Szanto served 4 terms as Treasurer, 2009 through 2017.
- 'Past Chair disagreement: the AGM/2009 slides list Emil Volcheck as Past Chair for 2009-2011, while the AGM/2010, 2011 and 2013 slides list Mark Giesbrecht.'
- 'CCA editors: Kotsireas and Lobo at the start (AGM/2009). The 2010 report says Austin Lobo stepped down and was replaced by Manuel Kauers (RISC, Linz), whose editorial-board seat went to Lihong Zhi. The 2013 report says Kotsireas steps down as co-editor from issue 185, with Kauers continuing as editor.'
- 'Advisory Board members at large, from the AGM/2010 slides (same list in AGM/2011, there marked ''Dormant''), with terms: Lichtblau 2009-2012; Kauers, May, Gao 2009-12; Dumas 2007-11; Watt, Sasaki, Wang 2008-11; Monagan 2007-10; von zur Gathen 2006-10. The AGM/2009 slides (''Current Advisory Board'') list instead Cooperman, Li, Labahn, Gonzalez-Vega (2005-9), von zur Gathen (2006-9), Dumas, Monagan, Watt, Sasaki, Wang, and propose Lichtblau, Kauers, May and Gao. The 2011-2013 reports say the advisory board had not been active for four years.'
- Affiliations are as abbreviated on the AGM slides. Only Johnson (Chair) and Szanto (Treasurer) are named in the 2010-2013 annual reports.
- 'The 2013 report: Mark Giesbrecht chaired the nominating committee for the 2013 elections.'
