Recently the [first call for papers](http://issac-conference.org/2020/calls/first-call-for-papers.txt) for [ISSAC 2020](http://issac-conference.org/2020/) was made. The conference takes place in Kalamata, Greece on 20-23 July 2020.

In the week before ISSAC the conference [ACA 2020](https://aca2020.sba-research.org/) takes place in Athens, Greece (July 15-18). The [call for special sessions](https://aca2020.sba-research.org/call-for-sessions.html) was recently made.
