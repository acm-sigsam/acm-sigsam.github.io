SIGSAM is happy to sponsor the [2017 East Coast Computer Algebra Day](https://www.sigsam.org/ECCAD/2017/).

The event, taking place at Wolfram Research in Champaign, Illinois, will include invited speakers: James H. Davenport, Bruce Reznick, Ekaterina Shemyakova and Dan Steffy. There will also be a contributed poster session. [Click here to read the Call for Participation](https://www.sigsam.org/ECCAD/2017/ECCAD-Call.txt).
