Nominations are now being accepted for the inaugural SIAG/Algebraic Geometry Early Career Prize.

The prize is awarded every two years to one outstanding early career researcher in the field of algebraic geometry and its applications. For further details including the eligibility criteria see [the award page](http://www.siam.org/prizes/sponsored/agearlycareer.php).
