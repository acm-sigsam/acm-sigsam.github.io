Issue 211 (Vol 54:1) of ACM Communications in Computer Algebra (CCA) is now available.

It can be accessed via the ACM Digital Library [here](http://dl.acm.org/sigsam/newsletter/current). This requires an ACM DL subscription (provided with SIGSAM membership).

The issue contains an article by J. van der Hoeven and G. Lecerf entitled *Ultimate complexity for numerical algorithms* and an obituary for Jacques Calmet.
