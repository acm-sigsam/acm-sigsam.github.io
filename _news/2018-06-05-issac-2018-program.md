[ISSAC 2018](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2018/) will take place in New York City, USA on 16-19 July. The program includes 47 accepted papers ([full list here](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2018/papers.php)); invited talks from Micha Sharir, Andrew Sommese and Thomas Sturm ([abstracts here](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2018/invited.php)); and tutorials from Shivkumar Chandrasekaran, Steve Linton, and Daniel Roche ([abstracts here](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2018/tutorials.php)). Registration is now open.
