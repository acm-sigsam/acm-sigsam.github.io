SIGSAM maintains a list of [Upcoming Conferences](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html). [ISSAC 2016](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2016/) will take place in Waterloo, Canada on 19-22 July. Other meetings include:

- 27-29 June 2016: [ADG 2016](http://icube-web.unistra.fr/adg2016/index.php/Accueil) in Strasbourg, France.
- 11-14 July 2016: [ICMS 2016](http://icms2016.zib.de/) in Berlin, Germany.
- 25-29 July 2016: [CICM 2016](http://www.cicm-conference.org/2016/cicm.php) in in Bialystok, Poland.
- 1-4 August 2016: [ACA 2016](http://www.mathematik.uni-kassel.de/ACA2016/) in Kassel, Germany.
- 19-23 September 2016: [CASC 2016](http://www.casc.cs.uni-bonn.de/) in Bucharest, Romania.
- 24-27 September 2016: [SYNASC 2016](http://synasc.ro/2016/) in Timisoara, Romania.

[Click here](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html) to view the full list. To include your meeting on these lists contact [Infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org](mailto:infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org).
