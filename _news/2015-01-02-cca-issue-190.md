Issue 190 of ACM Communications in Computer Algebra (CCA) is now available.

It won't appear on the digital library for a few weeks but SIGSAM members with an ACM web account can download the pdfs now from our [CCA archive](http://www.sigsam.org/cca/issues/index.html).

The issue includes: abstracts from the ISSAC 2014 Software Presentations; an article by Abramov and Barkatou on linear differential and difference operators; and an article by Monagan and Pearce on Maple's polynomial data structures.
