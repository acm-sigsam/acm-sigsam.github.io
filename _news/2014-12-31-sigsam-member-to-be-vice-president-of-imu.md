SIGSAM Member [Prof. Alicia Dickenstein](http://mate.dm.uba.ar/~alidick/) from the University of Buenos Aires, Argentina, has been elected Vice President of the IMU for the term 2015-2018.

The [International Mathematical Union (IMU)](http://www.mathunion.org/) is an international organization to promote international cooperation in mathematics.
