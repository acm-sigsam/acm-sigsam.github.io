ACM OpenTOC is a service for ACM Special Interest Groups to open the content from recently held conferences. It enables visitors to download content from the ACM Digital Library at no charge for a one year period following the conference start date. SIGSOFT have recently compiled a list ([available here](http://www.sigsoft.org/opentoc/opentoc.html)) of all 2014 OpenTOC papers.
