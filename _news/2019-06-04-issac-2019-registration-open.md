[ISSAC 2019](http://www.issac-conference.org/2019/index.php) has issued its [Call for Participation](http://www.issac-conference.org/2019/CallForParticipation.txt) and [Online Registration](http://www.issac-conference.org/2019/Registration.php) is now open.

Also announced are the [accepted papers](http://www.issac-conference.org/2019/papers.php), the [accepted software demonstrations](http://www.issac-conference.org/2019/software.php), and the [accepted poster presentations](http://www.issac-conference.org/2019/posters.php).
