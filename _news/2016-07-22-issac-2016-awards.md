The following awards were sponsored by ACM SIGSAM and presented at the ISSAC 2016 banquet last night.

- **Distinguished Paper Award**: Pierre Lairez and Tristan Vaccon - On p-adic differential equations with separation of variables
- **Distinguished Student Author Award**: Yi Zhang - Contraction of Ore Ideals with Applications
- **Distinguished Student Author Award**: Matias Bender - Contraction of Ore Ideals with Applications
