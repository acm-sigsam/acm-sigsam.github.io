The Proceedings of the 40th International Symposium in Symbolic and Algebraic Computation are now available online. SIGSAM members can access them from the ACM Digital Library [at this link](http://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=2755996).

The proceedings include 43 contributed papers selected by the Program Committee and invited papers from the 3 invited speakers and 3 tutors.
