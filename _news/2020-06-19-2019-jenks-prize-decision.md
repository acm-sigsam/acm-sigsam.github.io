The [2019 Richard D. Jenks Memorial Prize](/awards/jenks-prize/2019-stillman/) for Excellence in Software Engineering Applied to Computer Algebra has been awarded to Michael Stillman for the Macaulay and Macaulay2 computer algebra systems.

Macaulay, released 1983, was the first CAS devoted to Groebner basis computation while Macaulay2, released 1996, included a new user level language designed to read more like mathematics. Over 2000 papers on arXiv refer to Macaulay2 and since 2006 there have been over 200 Macaulay2 packages developed.

The award will be presented as part of [ISSAC 2020](http://www.issac-conference.org/2020/).
