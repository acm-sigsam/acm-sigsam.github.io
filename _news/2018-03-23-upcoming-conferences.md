SIGSAM maintains a list of [Upcoming Conferences](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html). [ISSAC 2018](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2018/) will take place in New York City, USA on 16-19 July. Other meetings include:

- 18-22 June 2018: [ACA 2018](https://www.usc.es/regaca/aca2018/) in Santiago de Compostela, Spain.
- 24-27 July 2018: [ICMS 2018](http://icms-conference.org/2018/) in South Bend, Indiana, USA.
- 17-21 September 2018: [CASC 2018](http://www.casc.cs.uni-bonn.de/2018/) in Lille, France.
- 20-23 September 2018: [SYNASC 2018](https://synasc.ro/2018/) in Timisoara, Romania.

[Click here](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html) to view the full list. To include your meeting on these lists contact [Infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org](mailto:infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org).
