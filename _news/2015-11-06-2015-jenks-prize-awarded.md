The 6th Richard D. Jenks Memorial Prize for Software Engineering Applied to Computer Algebra was awarded at the Fields Institute in Toronto during the [Major Thematic Program on Computer Algebra](https://www.fields.utoronto.ca/programs/scientific/15-16/computeralgebra/) to Professor Victor Shoup for NTL: A Library for doing Number Theory. See [the award page](/awards/jenks-prize/2015-shoup/) for details.
