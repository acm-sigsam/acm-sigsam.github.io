The [2017 Richard D. Jenks Memorial Prize](/awards/jenks-prize/2017-wolfram/) was awarded to Stephen Wolfram for Wolfram\|Alpha and Mathematica. The plaque was presented in person at the ISSAC 2018 banquet in New York on 18th July 2018.
