SIGSAM now has a Twitter account [@acm_sigsam]()! This will tweet SIGSAM news and announcments; and retweet stories of interest to the Symbolic Computation community.

The Chief SIGSAM Tweeter is [Alexander Konovalov](https://www.cs.st-andrews.ac.uk/directory/person?id=alexk) from The University of St Andrews, UK.
