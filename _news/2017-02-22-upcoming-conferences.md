SIGSAM maintains a list of [Upcoming Conferences](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html). [ISSAC 2017](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2017/) will take place in Kaiserslautern, Germany on 25-28 July. Other meetings include:

- 12-16 June 2017: [MEGA 2017](https://mega2017.inria.fr/) in Nice, France.
- 10-19 July 2017: [FoCM 2017](http://www.ub.edu/focm2017/index.html) in Barcelona, Spain.
- 17-21 July 2017: [ACA 2017](http://www.aca2017.jct.ac.il/) in Jerusalem, Israel.
- 23-24 July 2017: [PASCO 2017](http://www.sigsam.org/PASCO/2017/) in Kaiserslautern, Germany.
- 31 July - 4 August 2017: [SIAM AG:17](http://www.siam.org/meetings/ag17/) in Atlanta, GA, USA
- 18-22 September 2016: [CASC 2017](http://www.casc.cs.uni-bonn.de/2017/) in Beijing, China.
- 21-24 September 2016: [SYNASC 2017](http://synasc.ro/2017/) in Timisoara, Romania.

[Click here](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html) to view the full list. To include your meeting on these lists contact [Infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org](mailto:infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org).
