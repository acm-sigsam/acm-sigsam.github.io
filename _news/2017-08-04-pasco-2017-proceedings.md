PASCO 2017 took place at the University of Kaiserslautern, Kaiserslautern, Rheinland-Pfalz, Germany.

The proceedings can be found in the [ACM Digital Library](http://www.dx.doi.org/10.1145/3115936) and contain all papers presented at the conference. The event was run in cooperation with SIGSAM.
