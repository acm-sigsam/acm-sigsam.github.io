Following the worldwide pandemic of COVID19 there have been many effects on academic conferences. [FoCM 2020](http://focm-society.org/2020/) has been cancelled, [ACA 2020](https://aca2020.sba-research.org/virus-updates.html) will be postponed, [ICMS 2020](http://www.iaa.tu-bs.de/AppliedAlgebra/ICMS2020/ICMS2020.html) will be help online. A decision on [ISSAC 2020](http://issac-conference.org/2020/) is expected by the end of April. Check conference websites for the latest details.
