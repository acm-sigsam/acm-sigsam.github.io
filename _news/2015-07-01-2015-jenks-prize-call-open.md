The [Call for Nominations](/awards/jenks-prize/) for the 6th Richard D. Jenks Prize for Software Engineering Applied to Computer Algebra has been made. The prize will be awarded at [The Fields Institute](http://www.fields.utoronto.ca/programs/scientific/15-16/computeralgebra/) in October 2015.

All nominations must be received by the (extended) deadline of Friday September 4, 23:59pm EDT (+4 GMT). Further details and the nomination form are available [at this link](/awards/jenks-prize/).
