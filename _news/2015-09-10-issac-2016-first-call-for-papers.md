The 41st International Symposium in Symbolic and Algebraic Computations (ISSAC 2016) will be held at Wilfrid Laurier University, Waterloo, Ontario, Canada on 20-22 July 2016. The [ISSAC 2016 website](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2016/index.php) is now online.

The First Call for Papers has been made and [can be read as a text file here](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2016/calls_for_papers/first_call_for_papers.txt).
