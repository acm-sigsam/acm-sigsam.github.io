Issue 212 (Vol 54:2) of ACM Communications in Computer Algebra (CCA) is now available.

It can be accessed via the ACM Digital Library [here](http://dl.acm.org/sigsam/newsletter/current). This requires an ACM DL subscription (provided with SIGSAM membership).

The issue contains an article by Abramov, Khmelnov, and Ryabenko on *Checkpoints in searching for rational solutions of linear ordinary difference and differential systems* and the ISSAC 2020 Poster Abstracts.
