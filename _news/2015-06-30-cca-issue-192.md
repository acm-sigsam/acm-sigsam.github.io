Issue 192 of ACM Communications in Computer Algebra (CCA) is now available.

It won't appear on the digital library for a few weeks but SIGSAM members with an ACM web account can download the pdfs now from our [CCA archive](http://www.sigsam.org/cca/issues/index.html).

The issue includes: abstracts from the ACA 2014 Conference and a paper on Partitioning of Algebraic Subexpressions in Computer Algebra Systems.
