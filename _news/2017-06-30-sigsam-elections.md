Elections have taken place for the new SIGSAM executive. The results are [published by the ACM here](https://www.acm.org/elections/sigs/2017/sigsam-2017-results) and the [Committee page](/board/) is updated.

The new SIGSAM Chair is Prof. Chris Brown from the US Naval Academy. Also elected are: vice-Chair Lihong Zhi; Secretary Clement Pernet; and Treasurer Dan Roche.
