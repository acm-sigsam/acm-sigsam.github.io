The following SIGSAM sponsored awards were presented at the ISSAC 2015 Banquet, held in the Roman Baths, Bath, UK.

- Distinguished Paper Award: Jean-Guillaume Dumas, Clement Pernet and Ziad Sultan - Computing the rank profile matrix
- Distinguished Student Author Award: Sebastien Maulat - Formulas for Continued Fractions: An Automated Guess and Prove Approach ((with Bruno Salvy)

The full citations can be found on the [ISSAC Awards Page](/awards/).
