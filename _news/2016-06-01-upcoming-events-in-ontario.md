[ISSAC 2016](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2016) takes place this year at Wilfrid Laurier University Canada. [Registration](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2016/registration.php) is now open. Several workshops are being organised in Ontario to coincide with the event.

- 16-18 July: [Milestones in Computer Algebra](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/conferences/mica2016/).
- 19 July: [ISSAC 2016 Tutorial Day](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2016/tutorials.php).
- 20-22 July: [ISSAC 2016 Main Conference Days](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2016).
- 23-24 July: [The Waterloo Workshop on Computer Algebra](http://bohr.wlu.ca/ezima/WWCA2016/index.html).
- 25-28 July: [Celebrating 20 years of the Lambert W function](http://www.apmaths.uwo.ca/~djeffrey/LambertW/LambertW.html).

SIGSAM maintains a list of [Upcoming Conferences](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html). To include your meeting contact [Infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org](mailto:infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org).
