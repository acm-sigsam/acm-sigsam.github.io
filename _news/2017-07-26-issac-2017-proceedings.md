ISSAC 2017 is currently taking place at the University of Kaiserslautern, Kaiserslautern, Rheinland-Pfalz, Germany.

For those not in Kaiserslautern, [the proceedings can be found in the ACM Digital Library](http://dl.acm.org/citation.cfm?id=3087604) and contain all papers presented at the conference. The extended abstracts for poster presentations and software demonstrations will appear in a later issue of [Communications in Computer Algebra](http://www.sigsam.org/cca/).
