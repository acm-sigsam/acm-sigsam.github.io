A new SIGSAM Committee has been elected for the term 1st July 2021 - 30th June 2023: [Election Results](https://www.acm.org/elections/sigs/2021/sigsam-2021-results).

[Veronika Pillwein](https://risc.jku.at/m/veronika-pillwein/) (Research Institute for Symbolic Computation Johannes Kepler University, Austria) will be Chair, [Clement Pernet](https://www-ljk.imag.fr/membres/Clement.Pernet/) (Université Grenoble Alpes, France) Vice Chair, [Shaoshi Chen](http://www.mmrc.iss.ac.cn/~schen/) (Chinese Academy of Sciences, China) Secretary, and [Matthew England](https://matthewengland.coventry.domains/) (Coventry University, UK) Treasurer.

Zafeirakis Zafeirakopoulos (Gebze Technical University, Turkey) has been appointed as the new Information Director.
