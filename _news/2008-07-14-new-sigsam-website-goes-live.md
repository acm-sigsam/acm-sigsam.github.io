## The new SIGSAM web space

The SIGSAM website has been given a big facelift and had oodles of new material added. While this is still a work in progress, there is just too much here to keep hidden, including:

- Google calendars of computer algebra conferences, events and deadlines (continually updated).
- An archive of computer algebra conferences going back to the early 1970's
- A catalogue of journals (with links) publishing papers in computer algebra
- Up-to-date announcements (and pictures!) of awards and prizes.
- Posting of new Ph.D. abstracts, with an RSS feed.
- Job postings of positions in computer algebra.
- Computer algebra software, with a consistent citation policy.

Even more content is in store, so please check this site regularly (or subscribe to the newsfeeds for even more timely information).

The grand old site is still around at [here](http://www.sigsam.org/oldsite) if you need it. Please let us know if there is anything you need from there and we will incorporate it into the new site.
