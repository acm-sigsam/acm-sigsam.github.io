SIGSAM maintains a list of [Upcoming Conferences](http://www.sigsam.org/Resources/Upcoming.html). The SIGSAM sponsored [ISSAC 2015](http://www.issac-symposium.org/2015/) will take place in Bath, UK on 6-9 July. Other meetings this year include:

- 15-19 June 2015: [MEGA 2015](http://mega2015.science.unitn.it/) in Povo (Trento), Italy.
- 10-12 July 2015: [PASCO 2015](http://pasco2015.imag.fr/) in Bath, UK.
- 13-17 July 2015: [CICM 2015](http://www.cicm-conference.org/2015/cicm.php) in Washington DC, USA.
- 19-25 July 2015: [CoCoA15](http://www.math.colostate.edu/~betten/COCOA15/cocoa15.html) in Fort Collins, Colorado, USA.
- 20-23 July 2015: [ACA 2015](http://www.singacom.uva.es/ACA2015/) in Kalamata, Greece.
- 3-7 August 2015: [SIAM AG:15](http://camp.nims.re.kr/activities/eventpages/?id=200&action=overview) in Daejeon, South Korea.
- 14-18 September 2015: [CASC 2015](http://www.casc.cs.uni-bonn.de/) in Kassel, Germany.

To include your meeting on these lists contact [Infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org](mailto:infodir_SIGSAM@acm.org).
