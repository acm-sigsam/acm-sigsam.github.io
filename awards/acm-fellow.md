<p>
The <a href="http://fellows.acm.org">ACM Fellows Program</a> was established
by Council in 1993 to recognize and honor outstanding ACM members for their
achievements in computer science and information technology and for their
significant contributions to the mission of the ACM. The <a
href="http://fellows.acm.org">ACM Fellows</a> serve as distinguished
colleagues to whom the ACM and its members look for guidance and leadership as
the world of information technology evolves.
</p>
