SIGSAM is the largest international society for computer algebra and symbolic computation. Our mandate is to promote the science and application of computer algebra and symbolic computation, and to support those working in the field. In particular, we publish a long running (since 1965) newsletter [Communications in Computer Algebra]({{ '/cca/' | relative_url }}) (formerly the SIGSAM Bulletin); sponsor a number of [awards]({{ '/awards/' | relative_url }}) and conferences (including ISSAC, PASCO and SNC); and run our website to provide both the latest news, and archive material of interest to the symbolic computation community.

## Member Benefits

Benefits of being a SIGSAM member include:

- A subscription to the quarterly publication [Communications in Computer Algebra]({{ '/cca/' | relative_url }}): both in print and through member-only access to the pdf articles on the CCA website.
- A discount on registration at the annual [International Symposium on Symbolic and Algebraic Computation](https://www.issac-conference.org/) (ISSAC) which is usually in excess of the SIGSAM membership fee.
- Access to the full [ISSAC Proceedings](http://dl.acm.org/event.cfm?id=RE240&tab=pubs).
- Announcements of news and events in the field of symbolic computation through the SIGSAM-Members mailing list.
- Access to the [ACM Digital Library](http://dl.acm.org/).

## How to Join

SIGSAM membership costs a modest US$30 per year, or only US$9 per year for students.

- Current members can renew their membership [here](http://www.acm.org/renew).
- New members can join [here](https://services.acm.org/public/qj/gensigqj/gensigqj_control.cfm?promo=QJSIG&form_type=SIG&offering=024&byp=1?).

Alternatively, you can [print this form]({{ '/assets/join/SIGSAMMembershipForm.pdf' | relative_url }}), fill it in and return to the ACM.
