LIE and BIGLIE are standalone programs that run on any PC under MSDOS and do not require a seperate computer algebra system. LIE is the standard program and can find traditional point symmetries and also contact, Lie-Backlund, generalised and approximate symmetries. BIGLIE is for equations too big for LIE but is slower.
