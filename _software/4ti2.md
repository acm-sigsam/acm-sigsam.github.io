4ti2, by R. Hemmecke, R. Hemmecke, M. Köppe, P. Malkin and M. Walter, addresses algebraic, geometric and combinatorial problems on linear spaces, with commands for Markov, Gröbner and Graver bases, Hilbert bases, circuits, extreme rays, and integer solutions of linear systems (zsolve) (homepage and repository).
