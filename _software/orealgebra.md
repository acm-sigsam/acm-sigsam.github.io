ore_algebra is a SageMath package for Ore algebras, Ore polynomials and differentially finite functions, offering arithmetic, gcrd and lclm, D-finite closure properties, creative telescoping, guessing, desingularization and solvers for polynomial, rational and power series solutions (README). Univariate differential operators also support numerical computation of analytic solutions with rigorous error bounds; the package is by Manuel Kauers, Maximilian Jaroschek and Fredrik Johansson (setup.py), with Marc Mezzarobba and others among the main contributors (repository).
