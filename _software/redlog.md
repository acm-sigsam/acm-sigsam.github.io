Redlog (REDUCE Logic System), publicly available since 1995, is integrated with REDUCE and works with interpreted first-order logic over domains such as nonlinear real arithmetic, Presburger arithmetic and parametric quantified Boolean formulas, providing simplification, normal forms, quantifier elimination and decision procedures (homepage).
