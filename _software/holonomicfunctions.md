HolonomicFunctions, by Christoph Koutschan, computes annihilating ideals of multivariate holonomic functions and sequences, executes closure properties, and performs summation and integration via creative telescoping (RISC page). It also implements Ore algebra arithmetic, noncommutative Gröbner bases and solvers for coupled linear systems of differential or difference equations (RISC page).
