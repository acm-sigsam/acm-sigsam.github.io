FriCAS is an advanced computer algebra system, a fork (2007) of the Axiom computer algebra system (homepage). Its capabilities include polynomial and differential system solving, integration (the site calls it the most complete implementation of the Risch algorithm), series, Gröbner bases and guessing of formulas for sequences, and its distinguishing feature is its type system (Features page).
