FELIX is a special purpose CAS consisting of three layers: i) a small C-kernel, ii) a programming language including interpreter, compiler and linker, and iii) a module library of algebraic algorithms. The central application is Buchberger's algorithm with emphasise on its extensions to non-commutative rings and modules.
