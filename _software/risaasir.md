Risa/Asir is an open source general computer algebra system; the Kobe distribution is developed by the OpenXM committers, while the original Risa/Asir was developed at Fujitsu Labs (homepage). Risa/Asir is kept in the OpenXM_contrib2 tree, whose sources have been on GitHub since 2022 (OpenXM home page).
