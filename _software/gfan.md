Gfan, written by Anders Nedergaard Jensen, computes Gröbner fans and tropical varieties, the polyhedral fans associated with polynomial ideals: it computes all marked reduced Gröbner bases of an ideal (whose union is a universal Gröbner basis) and contains algorithms for tropical varieties, with specialized methods for tropical curves, hypersurfaces and prime ideals (homepage).
