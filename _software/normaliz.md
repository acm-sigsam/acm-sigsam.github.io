Normaliz computes, among other things, convex hulls and dual cones, triangulations, Hilbert bases of rational cones, normalizations of affine monoids, lattice points of polytopes and polyhedra, volumes, and Hilbert (Ehrhart) series; it also handles algebraic polyhedra defined over real algebraic extensions of Q, and offers the C++ library libnormaliz and interfaces to CoCoA, GAP, Macaulay2, polymake, Python and Singular (homepage).
