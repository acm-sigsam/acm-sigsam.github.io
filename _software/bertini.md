Bertini, by Daniel J. Bates, Jonathan D. Hauenstein, Andrew J. Sommese and Charles W. Wampler, is a general-purpose homotopy continuation solver written in C; it finds isolated solutions with adaptive multiprecision, implements parameter continuation, and treats positive-dimensional solution sets through witness sets and the numerical irreducible decomposition (homepage).
