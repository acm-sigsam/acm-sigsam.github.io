citation:
  - "The Galois theory of linear differential equations with parameters is a very active research area. There is a great deal of theoretical work that has been already developed, but the actual complete algorithms are missing. Dreyfus showed how to compute such a Galois group for a parameterized second order linear differential equation in a special form, under some additional technical conditions. These conditions were later removed by Carlos Arreche, who is also the sole author of the paper that extends these results for all second-order linear differential equations with rational function coefficients. This algorithmic extension is not only original but is also non-trivial."
